# Plan: Minecraft-style art

Working plan for the user's request of 2026-11-14 ("make the art much better ... rather than using your own art styles") and the user's choice of 2026-11-21: **option B of [ADR 0092](adr/0092-minecraft-style-art.md), an original vanilla-style generator**; option A (recolouring the user's own textures) is not chosen. Written 2026-11-21 by the orchestrator at fa37af3. Decisions live in ADR 0092 D6–D17 (the main session's, vetoable); this file holds the facts, limits, waves and briefs B491–B502. Nothing here starts before the main session gives the go; nothing after Wave 0 starts before B491 has merged.

## In plain words

- **What changes.** Every picture MCSuperMods makes for you (the Creator's blocks, items, armour and mobs, Surprise me, the starters and examples, the AI helper's textures) is drawn the way Minecraft's own pictures are drawn: a few shades of each colour that lean warm in the light and cool in the shadow, light from the top left, clumpy texture instead of flat colour, a dark outline round items, bricks, boards, bark and leaves that look like what they are. They are MCSuperMods' own drawings, never copied from the game.
- **Draw it for me.** Every picture field gets a **Draw…** button beside **Make with AI**: pick what it is (a sword, planks, leaves), a colour, a finish, how rough it looks, then one of six variations, and **New looks** for six more. It works without a key and without the internet. The texture painter and **New texture…** can start from a drawn picture, and the Mob Studio can draw a skin that fits the mob's model.
- **Try it** draws stone, dirt, grass, sand, planks, logs and leaves in the same style when the game's own pictures are not available.
- **What it is not.** It does not copy, recolour or learn from Minecraft's textures; pictures already in a project are not redrawn by themselves; paintings keep their placeholder landscape; drawn pictures are 16 by 16 (not 32 by 32).
- **Order.** The art module and its rules first (B491), then the recipes in three families and the Draw dialog side by side (wave 1), then the switch of every flow and example, the Mob Studio, the AI helper and Try it (wave 2), then tests, the guide and the close.

## Limits

| # | Limit | Why | What users see | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Vanilla-like, not vanilla.** | MSM never copies, recolours or learns from Mojang's textures (ADR 0092 D6, D12). | Pictures in the same style, not the same pixels. | Paint, Picture to texture (ADR 0048) or Make with AI. |
| A2 | **Paintings keep their placeholder landscape.** | A painting is the user's own picture; no recipe. | The Painting flow's default picture is unchanged. | Choose picture…; follow-up. |
| A3 | **Vanilla mob variant skins are an even coat.** | Their layout belongs to vanilla's models, which MSM does not carry. | A wolf or cat variant drawn by a flow has fur shading but no eyes or markings in place. | Paint it, or Picture to texture. |
| A4 | **Draw a skin needs box UV.** | Per-face UV gives no boxes to shade. | The Mob Studio's **Draw a skin** is greyed out with "This model's faces are mapped one by one". | Even coat via **Draw…** on the texture. |
| A5 | **Nothing is redrawn by itself.** | Never overwrite a user's pictures. | Old projects keep their placeholders until **Draw…** or Edit in Creator. | Guide paragraph. |
| A6 | **Hand-drawn fixture pictures stay placeholders.** | The golden tests draw some fixture inputs with `placeholderPng` directly. | Some sample fixture pictures are still flat. | Only flow-built pictures change (B497). |
| A7 | **16 by 16 only.** | Vanilla's size; 32 by 32 would need its own rules. | No high-resolution drawn pictures. | Follow-up. |
| A8 | **Animated drawings only for particles.** | The portal surface is one frame. | A drawn portal surface does not swirl. | Follow-up (a frame strip and `.mcmeta`). |

## Rules for every brief

- Read root `CLAUDE.md`, `packages/plugins/CLAUDE.md` or `app/CLAUDE.md` for the area, the latest `docs/DEVLOG.md` entry, ADR 0092 (D6–D17), ADR 0093 for anything beside **Make with AI**, and this plan's "Notes from implementation". Art has no version facts; if a brief finds one (a texture folder that differs between targets), it lives in `packages/targets`.
- **Clean room (ADR 0092 D12).** No brief opens, traces, measures, screenshots or compares against a Minecraft texture, model or GUI picture, and none reads the client jar or its extracted cache. Silhouettes and structures are drawn from the words in the brief and from the prototype. No vanilla picture goes into tests, goldens, the contact sheet, the guide or a commit. Comparisons with vanilla are the user's, on their own machine.
- **Determinism (D11).** No `Math.random`, `Date` or locale in `art/`; seeds through `hashSeed`; PNGs through `encodePng`. The same look gives the same bytes.
- **Goldens.** A brief updates only its own golden folder under `packages/plugins/library/test/fixtures/art/` with `UPDATE_GOLDENS=1`. Only the main session regenerates `docs/reference/art/sheet.png` (`UPDATE_ART_SHEET=1 pnpm --filter @msm/plugin-library test`, added by B491), after each wave is merged. Only B497 updates the `examples/` mirror, the sample fixtures and the exporters' pinned hashes.
- **No new dependency** in the library or the app. A brief that wants one stops and reports.
- **Git.** Each executor works in its own git worktree in the scratchpad (`<scratchpad>/wt-B<nnn>`, from the commit the orchestrator names), builds and tests there, commits on its own branch, does not push and does not merge. Commits are made with `GIT_AUTHOR_NAME=Rakib GIT_AUTHOR_EMAIL=rakib1insta@gmail.com GIT_COMMITTER_NAME=Rakib GIT_COMMITTER_EMAIL=rakib1insta@gmail.com` in the environment. **Never run `git config`.** Commit messages carry no `Co-Authored-By`, `Claude-Session` or other credit or trailer lines (the user's rule).
- **Disk.** `df -h` before building; stop below 10 GB free; delete `app/out`, `app/dist` and `node_modules/.cache` before reporting.
- **One owner per file per wave.** Parallel briefs never touch the same file; "Owner files" lines are complete and anything else is read-only. A brief that needs another's file stops and reports. Files of the Try it plan (A13) are touched only where a brief says so, after the main session has agreed it with that plan's running brief.
- **Exit criteria** always include `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `node tools/check-deps/index.ts` and the touched packages' tests; app briefs also `pnpm build` and `node tools/check-size/index.ts` (report `app.asar` and the renderer; stop and report above 31 MB or 17.5 MiB).
- **Notes.** Each executor ends its report with "Notes for the plan" (names, signatures, labels in words, test ids, timings, the hue shift chosen, what a later brief must change); the main session appends them to "Notes from implementation". Executors do not edit this file. Each executor appends one DEVLOG entry in its commit.
- **Plain words.** Labels are British English, no emojis, no ids in Very easy; they go into the notes for B501.
- **Models.** Executors run on Opus (`claude-opus-5-5`) at medium effort; B491 at high effort; B501 on Sonnet (`claude-sonnet-5-5`). A stuck brief is rerun on Opus at high effort.
