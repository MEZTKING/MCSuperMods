# ADR 0101: Vanilla parity 2: flows, Studios, templates, Try it and tests

## Context

ADRs [0094](0094-remaining-vanilla-block-types-waterlogging-and-weathering.md) to [0100](0100-dimension-types-and-skies.md) add twenty block types, waterlogging, weathering and waxing, item inventories and bundles, commands, creative tabs, two events through the runtime's first Mixins, Script API 1.6, Rules cards, vanilla's feature order, trader mobs, villager professions, mount storage, shoulder pets, fluids and dimension type editing. Earlier batches paired their format ADRs with one ADR for the app, the library and the tests ([ADR 0073](0073-mob-flows-templates-and-presets.md), [ADR 0081](0081-particle-studio-flows-and-tests.md), [ADR 0091](0091-vehicle-flows-studios-try-it-and-tests.md)); this is that ADR for both batches of "Vanilla parity 2". The renderer budget is 18 MiB since B407 (route b) and `app.asar` 27.7 of 32 MB; Try it (ADR 0085) is still in progress and owns `packages/sim/` and `app/src/renderer/src/tryit/` until its close.

## Decision

- **D1 Studios.** No new Studio for block types: the Block Studio's Type picker gains the twenty types in groups (Furniture and lights: bed, cake, ladder, torch, lantern, chain, candle, rod, carpet, scaffolding, sign, hanging sign; Plants: berry bush, tall plant, potted plant, vine, sea pickle, stem, cocoa, layers), with the type's own fields, a **Waterlogging** switch on the `MsmBlock` types and a **Weathering** section (the stage, the next block picked or created, the waxed block created with one click). The Item Studio gains an **Inventory** section (bag layout and size, or "works like a bundle"). New Studios: **Command** (name, arguments as rows with a type picker, permission in words, what it runs, a preview of the chat line `/name <arg>`), **Creative tab** (icon, title, the items that name it, drag to order), **Profession** (job site block, five levels of trades in the trade editor of the Trader section, textures), **Fluid** (textures with an animated preview, "Behaves like", flow sliders shown in blocks a second, bucket) and **Dimension type** (ADR 0100 D1). The Mob Studio gains **Trader**, **Storage** and **Shoulder** sections. Each Studio is lazy-loaded; each app brief reports its chunk size.
- **D2 Creator flows** (library starters with `creator`, ADR 0043): batch 1: **Copper set** (a block, cut block, slab, stairs, door, trapdoor and bars in four stages with waxed copies, honeycomb and axe rules, recipes; the number of pieces is a question), **Lights** (torch, lantern, chain and candle from one picture and one colour of light), **Bed** (a bed or a sleeping bag with `straw_bed`), **Cake** (a slice-by-slice cake or pie with its food values and a recipe), **Backpack** (a bag with rows, a recipe and an optional dye recipe), and answers added to existing flows: the Crop flow's "Bush with berries" and "Grows on" (soil), the Wood set's "Signs" and "Hanging signs" (default on), the Stone set's "Make it a copper-like set that ages" (off). **Custom command** and **Creative tab** are flows for the Creator's "Make your mod do something" group. Batch 2: **Wandering merchant** (a trader mob with a trade editor), **Village job** (a profession and its job site block), **Fluid** (a fluid, its bucket and an optional lake feature), the Mount flow's "Carries a chest" and the Pet flow's "Rides on your shoulder" (small pets only), and the Dimension flow's "Give it its own sky and rules" (ADR 0100 D2). Every flow's untouched defaults create a project with zero problems on both targets (proved in e2e).
- **D3 Templates and examples.** Templates (blocks-items, category "Blocks: furniture and lights", "Blocks: plants" and "Copper"): Sleeping bag, Pie, Berry bush, Tall flower, Rope ladder, Torch, Lantern, Chain, Candle, Wooden sign, Hanging sign, Potted sapling, Sea lantern pickle, Vine, Rug, Snow-like layers, Glow rod, Melon-like stem, Cocoa-like pod, Scaffold, Copper block (four stages), Waterlogged lamp; items: Satchel (27), Pouch (hopper), Quiver-like bundle; scripting: Help command, Teleport home command, Creative tab; entities: Wandering merchant, Pack mule, Pocket pet; worldgen: Moon dimension type, Nether-like dimension type; blocks-items: Honey-like fluid, Oil fluid. Library examples (golden in `examples/`): "Cottage furniture" (bed, lantern, signs, potted plants, a rug, a cupboard with storage), "Orchard and pie" (berry bush, tall flower, cake), "Copper roof" (weathering stairs and slabs, waxing), "Adventurer's satchel" (backpack, bundle, a creative tab), "Server helper" (commands with Rules), and in batch 2 "Roadside merchant" (trader, trade sets), "Village blacksmith job" (profession), "Oil well" (fluid, bucket, lake). Names are working names, original to MSM.
- **D4 Very easy.** No new tiles in batch 1: "Make a block" offers the new kinds of block as pictures (Lights, Bed, Cake, Sign, Plant with berries) and "Make an item" offers "A bag that holds things"; Rules gain the new cards in plain words. Batch 2 adds one tile, **Make a trader** ("A mob that trades with you."). No ids are shown.
- **D5 Try it** (after the Try it plan closes; otherwise the briefs move into it): the sim learns the new block states and their use (bites, harvesting, lighting candles, ladders and vines climbable, waterlogged looks, a weathering stage step in the time-lapse, sign text as a mock-up), item bags (a mock-up chest screen), commands typed in a chat box (logged in "What happened"), the place and explosion triggers, traders (a mock-up merchant screen with the offers), fluids (still and flowing pictures, no flow simulation, limit), and the dimension sky colour. Anything not simulated is a limit line in its brief and the guide.
- **D6 Tests.** Fixtures `fixtures/sample-vanilla-blocks` (batch 1) and `fixtures/sample-villagers-and-fluids` (batch 2), each with goldens for both targets and an in-game checklist README; parity test classes `SampleVanillaBlocksParityTest` and `SampleVillagersParityTest` on the content jar of both versions (and the ejected jar for batch 1's ejected features); client GameTests for drawing (signs with text, beds, torches and lanterns, a bundle tooltip, fluids still and flowing, a villager in a project profession) with screenshots in the artefact and `assertScreenshotContains` where a colour can be pinned; e2e paths `vanilla-blocks-path`, `backpack-path`, `commands-path` (batch 1) and `trader-path`, `fluid-path` (batch 2); the CI server check pins the new log lines and forbids `NoClassDefFoundError` and `Mixin apply failed`.
- **D7 AI helper.** Schema-summary allowances and system prompt paragraphs for the new types, components and kinds; no new tools; scripted build scenarios "a cottage with a bed and lanterns" and "a merchant who sells copper".
- **D8 Eject.** Batch 1's block types, waterlogging, weathering, item inventories, commands and creative tabs are ejected (26.3); batch 2's features warn `export.not-in-eject`.

## Consequences

- Two batches, each ending with the proof earlier batches had: a fixture, parity on both versions, client GameTests, e2e paths and a guide page (`blocks-and-items.md`, `block-types-and-plants.md`, new `commands-and-tabs.md`, `villagers-and-traders.md`, `fluids.md`, `worldgen.md` additions).
- Very easy gains one tile (its fifteenth with vehicles' tile).
