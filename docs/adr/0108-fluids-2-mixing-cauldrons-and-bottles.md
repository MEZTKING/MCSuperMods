# ADR 0108: Fluids 2: mixing, cauldrons and bottles

## Context

[ADR 0099](0099-custom-fluids.md) D5 left out, among others, mixing rules, cauldrons, bottles, pipes and tanks, buoyancy other than water's, boats on project fluids, fog colours on 26.3 and waterlogging by a project fluid ([ADR 0101](0101-vanilla-parity-2-flows-studios-try-it-and-tests.md) limit Q25). Filtered by the user's rule (vanilla has it): vanilla has mixing (lava and water make obsidian, cobblestone or stone; lava over soul soil next to blue ice makes basalt), cauldrons (water, lava, powder snow) and bottles (the water bottle). Vanilla has no pipes or tanks, its only buoyancies are water's and lava's (which `behaves_like` gives), and only water waterlogs: those are dropped. Fog colours are client rendering (the next client batch). Boats on a water-like fluid and resource packs retexturing a fluid may already work through the fluid tag and the texture paths: the spike checks (X28, X29). At f6a8adc the runtime has no cauldron, bottle or mixing code (fact H13); 26.3 lava-like fluids mix by vanilla's rule and 26.4 does not (ADR 0099 findings).

## Decision

- **D1 Mixing.** `msm:fluid` gains `mixing?: [{ with: "water" | "lava" | <fluid id>, source?: <block>, flowing?: <block> }]`: when this fluid meets the other one, the source or flowing block it touches becomes the named block (vanilla's obsidian-versus-cobblestone distinction is the `source` and `flowing` pair; limit N16), with vanilla's fizz sound and smoke particles. Our own liquid block's checks do it on both versions (X25), replacing the 26.3-only vanilla behaviour for lava-like fluids when `mixing` is given and making 26.3 and 26.4 behave alike.
- **D2 Cauldrons.** `msm:fluid` gains `cauldron?: { levels?: boolean }`: a companion block `<path>_cauldron` (vanilla's cauldron model with the fluid's still picture and tint; three levels like water's, or full only like lava's), filled and emptied by the fluid's bucket and, with `levels`, by its bottle, with vanilla's sounds; vanilla's empty cauldron takes the bucket through its interaction map (X26). Entities inside get `on_entity_inside` as in the fluid.
- **D3 Bottles.** `msm:fluid` gains `bottle?: { food?, effects?, on_drink? }`: a companion item `<path>_bottle`, filled by a glass bottle used on a source (or a levelled cauldron), drunk like a potion with the given food, effects and event, leaving a glass bottle (X27).
- **D4 Checked, not built unless needed:** boats on water-like project fluids (X28) and resource packs retexturing a project fluid (X29); if either fails, a fix goes into B529 or B531, else a guide line says it works.
- **D5 Dropped:** pipes, tanks, other buoyancies, waterlogging by a project fluid (not vanilla); fog colours on 26.3 (client batch, limit N18).
- **D6 Target facts.** Plugin API 1.20.0: `BlockFacts.cauldrons` (vanilla's cauldron ids, for the validator's id clash check).

## Consequences

- Fluids reach vanilla's level of interaction; the Fluid flow asks three new questions.
- Two companions per fluid at most (cauldron, bottle; limit N2).
- Mixing behaves the same on both versions once `mixing` is set; without it the old per-version behaviour stays (stated in the guide).

## Amendment after the spike (B525, 2026-11-22)

Measured on both versions (evidence in `docs/reference/spikes/b525/`); these win over D1 to D4 where they differ.

- **D1 mixing: go on both versions, by the liquid block's own check.** The liquid block overrides `onPlace` and `neighborChanged`, looks at the five neighbours other than the one below, and on a match sets the rule's source or flowing block with the lava fizz (`LevelEvent.LAVA_FIZZ`). When `mixing` is set it never calls vanilla's code (which on 26.3 would still make obsidian) and schedules the fluid tick itself. Fluid-id rules are checked before tag rules (a project fluid in `#minecraft:water` would otherwise match the tag first). Liquids are compared by their legacy block (`Fluid.isSame` exists on 26.3 only). Lava falling into water (vanilla's stone) is optional, by a `spreadTo` override in the `MsmFluid` seam.
- **D2 cauldrons: go, no Mixin.** A project cauldron subclasses vanilla's layered or full-only cauldron with an empty interaction dispatcher (`Dispatcher.put` is not public) and its own `useItemOn` over vanilla's public `fillBucket`/`emptyBucket` helpers; vanilla's empty cauldron takes a project bucket or bottle through `UseBlockCallback`. The layered class puts out burning entities and uses a level each time, so only water-like fluids use it unchanged; lava-like cauldrons are full-only.
- **D3 bottles: go.** Vanilla's glass bottle fills with water from any `#minecraft:water` fluid, so the project bottle needs a `UseItemCallback` before vanilla's; the bottle is a drink (`Consumable` with the drink animation, 1.6 s default, `usingConvertsTo` the glass bottle), `finishUsingItem` the `on_drink` hook.
- **D4 boats and packs.** Vanilla boats float and glide on water-like project fluids with no change (on 26.4 a boat loses speed near the edge of a source area: verify in game). A resource pack can repaint a project fluid by replacing its picture files at their texture paths, but not by re-pointing the liquid block's model (read from the mod jar at client start).

## Findings at close (B544, 2026-11-22)

Recorded at the close of vanilla parity 3 batch 2 (B544) from the code on the session branch at a57dbd3 and the plan's notes: B525 (f9d3e39, the spike; amendment 233f397), B526 (a8a416b), B527 (04d9019), B529 (8017874, 36cac0e), B531 (c13fcf5), B537 (7a70225), B538 (054e871), B539 (851ff8e), B540 (074e78e), B541 (f565cf9) and B543 (66043fb). In the 1.5.3 test build; awaiting the user's in-game checks (`fixtures/sample-mobs-and-commands/README.md`).

- **Spike answers** (B525, both versions; `docs/reference/spikes/b525/`). **X25:** our liquid block's own check in `onPlace` and `neighborChanged` mixes identically on 26.3 and 26.4 (source and flowing results, fizz level event 1501, project fluid ids and vanilla's tags). To replace vanilla's 26.3-only obsidian for a lava-like fluid the block must not call vanilla's code there and schedules the fluid tick itself (measured: no obsidian, the fluid still spreads). A rule naming a fluid id must be checked before a rule naming a tag. Vanilla lava next to a water-like project fluid still makes obsidian on both versions (vanilla's own lava block decides). **X26:** `CauldronInteraction.Dispatcher.put` is not public, so a project cauldron overrides `useItemOn` over vanilla's public `fillBucket` and `emptyBucket`; vanilla's empty cauldron takes a project bucket or bottle through `UseBlockCallback`; no Mixin. **X27:** vanilla's glass bottle gives a water bottle from any fluid in `#minecraft:water`, so the project bottle needs a `UseItemCallback` before vanilla's; the bottle is a plain item with a drinking `Consumable`. **X28:** vanilla boats float on water-like project fluids on both versions (same height); on 26.4 a pushed boat stopped about 0.8 blocks sooner near the edge of the source area. **X29:** a resource pack replacing the pictures at the fluid's texture paths changes them; re-pointing the liquid block's model does not (ADR 0099's correction).
- **D1 as built** (B527, B529). `msm:fluid.mixing` holds 1 to 16 rules `{ with, source?, flowing? }` (`with` is `water`, `lava` or a fluid id; a rule needs `source` or `flowing`). `registry/fluid/FluidMixing` is called from `MsmLiquidBlock`'s own `onPlace` and `neighborChanged`: it looks at the five neighbours other than the one below, checks every id rule (in file order) before every tag rule, compares liquids by their legacy block, sets the rule's block with `LevelEvent.LAVA_FIZZ` and, when `mixing` is set, never calls vanilla's mixing. Both `MsmFluid` seams have the optional `spreadTo` for the liquid falling into another. Unknown ids warn at `SERVER_STARTING`. Without `mixing` the old per-version behaviour stays (26.3 lava-like fluids mix by vanilla's rule, 26.4 do not).
- **D2 as built** (B529, B531). `cauldron { levels? }`: the companion block `<path>_cauldron` is `MsmCauldronBlock.Layered` (levels 1 to 3, water-like or `none` fluids) or `.Full` (full only; always for a lava-like fluid, where `levels` is ignored with a warning). Vanilla's cauldron classes with an empty dispatcher and their own `useItemOn`; a bucket takes only a full cauldron, a bottle takes or adds one level, the comparator reads the level; a water-like layered cauldron puts out burning entities and uses a level each time (vanilla's layered behaviour); both fire the fluid's `on_entity_inside` with its cooldown, drop vanilla's cauldron (vanilla's loot table) and leave vanilla's empty cauldron when emptied. A bucket of another liquid replaces the contents, as vanilla's cauldrons do among themselves. Lowering puts levels 1 to 3 on vanilla's `template_cauldron_*` parents. **Not as D2 says:** the contents are drawn untinted (there is no block colour provider for the cauldron), so a tinted fluid's cauldron shows the still picture's own colour; the client GameTest screenshot records it and the fixture README asks the user to look. A follow-up.
- **D3 as built.** `bottle { food?, effects?, on_drink? }`: the companion item `<path>_bottle` is `MsmBottleItem` (stacks to 16, a 1.6 s drink, `usingConvertsTo` the glass bottle, `on_drink` after food and effects), filled by a glass bottle on a source through `UseItemCallback` or from a levelled cauldron; lowered with the potion look tinted and the name "Bottle of <name>".
- **D4 outcome.** Boats float on water-like project fluids with no change (the 26.4 slowing near a source's edge is a guide line and an in-game check). A resource pack repaints a project fluid by replacing its picture files; both are in `docs/guide/fluids.md`.
- **D5 holds.** Pipes, tanks, other buoyancies and waterlogging by a project fluid stay out (not vanilla); fog colours on 26.3 wait for the client batch (limit N18).
- **D6 as built.** `BlockFacts.cauldrons` holds vanilla's four cauldron ids; the validator refuses a clash.
- **Validators and Studio** (B531, B538). Mixing codes `FLUID_MIXING_PROBLEM_CODES` (4) with the fluid checks, cauldron codes (2) in `msm.blocks-items.cauldrons`; the Fluid Studio's **Mixing** and **Cauldron and bottle** sections; `bottle.effects` is edited under Advanced only. The registry index knows `<id>_cauldron` and `<id>_bottle` (`fluidCompanionIds`), and vanilla packs list both as mod-only.
- **`MsmRegistrar.checkEvents` is public** (ADR 0099's follow-up), so fluid, cauldron and bottle events get the block event checks at start. Fluid events still cannot be changed by a dev-mode overlay (`DevFilesTest` keeps `fluid` as its one exception).
- **Tests.** `MsmFluid2GameTest` on both versions (each mixing pair, source and flowing; cauldron levels by bucket and bottle; a bottle drunk with its own `on_drink`); the GameTest world logs `registered 3 fluid cauldron(s) and 2 fluid bottle(s)`; six parity cases with maple syrup and hot tar on the content jars of both versions and the 26.3 ejected jar; the client GameTest shows vanilla's water cauldron beside a project one; three sim-parity scenarios recorded in the game agree with Try it.
- **Limits.** N16 (one block per meeting; source and flowing may differ) and N17 (three levels or whole; no potion-like mixtures) hold; N17's "in the fluid's own colour" does not yet (the untinted contents above). No powder snow is poured in (its empty sound differs between the versions); dispensers do not fill or empty project cauldrons or use project bottles; no rain or dripstone fills one. ADR 0101's limit Q25 is closed for what vanilla has.
- **Open follow-ups.** A block colour provider for the cauldron's contents; whether a water-like fluid's own `with: "water"` rule matches itself (an in-game check); the bottle's tint on a real client; a Studio editor for `bottle.effects`; dispensers on project cauldrons and bottles; fluid events in dev-mode overlays; the spectator skip in `on_entity_inside` (an in-game check).

## Status

Accepted with findings (2026-11-22, close B544; in the 1.5.3 test build, awaiting the user's in-game checks). The user has not yet been shown the defaults and may still veto them. Vetoable defaults as built: mixing only when listed, at most 16 rules, every id rule before every tag rule; a cauldron with three levels only when `levels` is set, and never for a lava-like fluid; a water-like levelled cauldron using a level each time it puts a fire out; a bucket of another liquid replacing a cauldron's contents; no powder snow, rain or dripstone into a project cauldron and no dispenser use; bottles only when `bottle` is set, stacking to 16 and drunk in 1.6 seconds; the companion names `<path>_cauldron` and `<path>_bottle`; cauldron contents drawn untinted until a colour provider exists. Proposed at first (2026-11-22) with these defaults: mixing only when listed; a cauldron with three levels only when `levels` is set; bottles only when `bottle` is set; the companion names `<path>_cauldron` and `<path>_bottle`.
