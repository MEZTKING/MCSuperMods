# ADR 0103: Growing and climbing plants, and project flower pots

## Context

After vanilla parity 2 MSM has 38 block types ([ADR 0094](0094-remaining-vanilla-block-types-waterlogging-and-weathering.md)), but none for the plants vanilla grows in strands or stalks: kelp, weeping and twisting vines, cave vines with glow berries, sugar cane, cactus, bamboo, big and small dripleaf and glow lichen ([ADR 0101](0101-vanilla-parity-2-flows-studios-try-it-and-tests.md) limit Q10; `loader/BlockType.java`, fact H1 of [ADR 0112's fact table](0112-vanilla-parity-3-flows-studios-eject-try-it-and-tests.md#fact-table-h1-to-h16-moved-from-the-plan-at-b544)). A potted plant can only go into vanilla's empty flower pot (`MsmPottedPlantBlock extends FlowerPotBlock`, limit Q5, fact H4); vanilla has the flower pot as a block in its own right, so a project pot that takes plants is a gap under the user's rule of 2026-11-14.

Vanilla groups these plants into a few shapes (verify at implementation, spike question X1): a head and body pair that grows one block at a time up or down (kelp, weeping and twisting vines, cave vines); a stalk that grows up to a height on given soils (sugar cane, cactus, and bamboo with its leaf stages and shoot); a block that sits on any face of its neighbours and spreads by bone meal (glow lichen); and the dripleaf pair (a tilting top on a stem, and a small two-block plant that grows into it).

## Decision

- **D1 Four new types instead of nine.** `type` gains:
  - `growing_plant`: fields `direction` (`up` or `down`), `max_age` (default 25, as kelp; verify at implementation), `growth_chance` (default 0.14; verify), `needs_water` (kelp-like: waterlogged by nature, survives only in water), `berries` (optional `{ item, light }`: cave-vine-like berries picked by use, the plant glowing at `light` while it has them), `climbable` (default true for vines, false for kelp), `soil` (block or tag ids the base needs; default none: any sturdy face). Registers the head under the element id and the body as the companion `<path>_plant` (vanilla's naming).
  - `column_plant`: fields `style` (`cane`, `cactus` or `bamboo`), `max_height` (default 3, as sugar cane and cactus; bamboo 16; verify), `soil` (default the vanilla tags of the style as `TargetFacts.blocks.plantSoils` lists them), `needs_water_nearby` (cane-like; default true for `cane`), `touch_damage` (cactus-like; default 1 for `cactus`, else 0), and for `bamboo` the shoot companion `<path>_shoot`.
  - `multiface`: glow-lichen-like; fields `light` (default 7, as glow lichen; verify), `spreads` (default true: bone meal spreads it), `waterloggable` (default true).
  - `dripleaf`: fields `size` (`big` or `small`); `big` registers the stem companion `<path>_stem`, tilts under a standing entity (vanilla's timing), can be grown taller with bone meal; `small` grows into the `big` element named by `grows_into`.
- **D2 Project flower pots.** A fifth new type, `flower_pot`, is an empty pot: field `plants`, the vanilla and project plants it takes (thing picker; ids of blocks with an item). For each listed plant the lowering adds a companion potted block `<pot path>_<plant path>` (vanilla's `flower_pot_cross` model with the pot's own texture and the plant's), and the runtime gives the pot its own plant-to-pot map, leaving vanilla's pot and its map alone (X4). `potted_plant` gains an optional `pot` (default vanilla's), so a project plant can be put into a project pot without being listed twice.
- **D3 Behaviour is vanilla's.** Each family extends the vanilla class the spike names (B503, X1) with the driver's events and triggers (ADR 0067 D3); growth, bone meal, survival, berries, tilting and spreading are vanilla's own behaviour over the definition's numbers. Nothing is transcribed from vanilla's code.
- **D4 Companions** follow ADR 0094 D3: reserved ids, one load report line, the validator refuses a clash (limit N2).
- **D5 Target facts.** Plugin API 1.19.0 adds `BlockFacts.plantSoils` (the vanilla tags each style survives on, from B503's observations) so the Studio and the validator offer them; version differences stay in the target.
- **D6 Not in this ADR.** Candle cakes on project cakes (vanilla pairs only its own cake); bamboo-like shapes other than vanilla's (limit N4); chorus-like plants (vanilla's chorus is an End feature with a fixed block; the existing `block` type with features covers its look but not its growth: a later batch if asked).

## Consequences

- The last everyday plants of vanilla are possible; the Creator gets "Cave plants" and "Desert plants" flows and a "Flower pot" flow ([ADR 0112](0112-vanilla-parity-3-flows-studios-eject-try-it-and-tests.md)).
- Five more types to keep on both versions; each has GameTests and parity cases.
- Project pots add one registered block per listed plant (the registry grows with the list; the Studio shows the count).

## Amendment after the spike (B503, 2026-11-22)

Measured on both versions (evidence in `docs/reference/spikes/b503/`). These win over D1 to D3 where they differ.

- **Classes.** `growing_plant` extends vanilla's kelp, weeping vine, twisting vine and cave vine head and body classes, overriding `getBodyBlock`/`getHeadBlock` (and for berries `useWithoutItem` and `getCloneItemStack`); our own `growth_chance` and bone-meal count use `GrowingPlantHeadBlock`/`GrowingPlantBodyBlock` directly (verify at implementation). `max_age` is the starting age within vanilla's fixed 0..25 range: a plant grows at most 26 blocks from age 0. `column_plant` cane and cactus use `SugarCaneBlock` and `CactusBlock` with `randomTicks()`; `multiface` uses `GlowLichenBlock`; light comes from the block's properties.
- **D3 changed for bamboo and dripleaf.** Vanilla's bamboo and dripleaf classes name vanilla's own blocks (a naked stalk has no height cap, a shoot and bone meal turn into vanilla blocks). We keep them in scope (vanilla parity) and the runtime writes its own height, leaf-stage, shoot and bone-meal rules for `column_plant` `bamboo` and for `dripleaf`, over the numbers measured in the spike (vanilla's bamboo stops at 12 to 16 with leaves none, small, large; dripleaf tilts after 2, 10 and 10 ticks), behind overrides of the members the spike lists (stalk `getHeightAboveUpToMax`, `getHeightBelowUpToMax`, `growBamboo`, `canSurvive`; shoot `growBamboo`, `updateShape`, `getCloneItemStack`; leaf and stem `performBonemeal`, `canSurvive`, `getCloneItemStack`). Nothing is transcribed from vanilla's code; the rules are written from the observations and checked by parity cases against vanilla.
- **Soil.** Vanilla's survival checks read shared tags (`#minecraft:supports_sugar_cane` and the others in `TargetFacts.blocks.plantSoils`). Adding a project soil to those tags would also change vanilla's plants, so a given `soil` list is checked by the element's own `canSurvive`; without `soil` the plant uses vanilla's tags. (Vetoable: tag additions instead.)
- **D2 pots.** A project pot is its own block class with its own plant-to-pot map, never a `FlowerPotBlock` (a `FlowerPotBlock` takes over vanilla's empty pot for that plant). The validator's `potted-plant-not-plant` warning says so plainly.
- **Cactus damage** other than vanilla's needs our own `entityInside`; the cactus flower was not seen in 20,000 forced ticks (verify at implementation).

## Findings at close (B523, 2026-11-22)

Recorded at the close of vanilla parity 3 batch 1 (B523) from the code on the session branch at 4ede504 and the plan's notes: B503 (0af2603, a937a69, the spike), B505 (a5e2619), B506 (92ed96f, 447146a), B507 (5149004), B509 (ee9fc7a), B510 (bd41469), B510b (1b75683), B511 (bcaf5bc), B512 (c529ef4, a2a324b), B515 (bc6f27e), B516 (6f544a0), B517 (9646bad), B518 (a8d50f5), B519 (3b94abc), B520 (c01b166), B521 (3e92e0a), B522 (a517f0c) and the merge findings (3cfb60e). Unreleased; awaiting the user's in-game checks (`fixtures/sample-plants-and-storage/README.md`).

- **Spike answers** (B503, observed on 26.3 and 26.4-snapshot-7; evidence and the spike source in `docs/reference/spikes/b503/`). **X1:** the kelp, weeping vine, twisting vine and cave vine head and body classes work as project blocks with `Properties` and the partner linked (`getBodyBlock` on the head, `getHeadBlock` on the body); cave vines also need `useWithoutItem` and `getCloneItemStack`, or they give glow berries. Sugar cane, cactus and glow lichen need no override. Bamboo and dripleaf name vanilla blocks in growth, bone meal and survival (a naked stalk has no cap, the shoot turns into vanilla bamboo, dripleaf bone meal places vanilla stems and leaves): the amendment above. **X2:** survival reads the `#minecraft:supports_*` tags; sugar cane's water check accepts water, a waterlogged block, a project fluid in `#minecraft:water`, frosted ice and `#minecraft:supports_sugar_cane_adjacently`. **X3:** a growing head is at most 26 blocks from age 0 (`AGE` 0..25 is static); kelp grows one block per bone meal; cave-vine berries are picked by use and their light goes out with them; cane and cactus need `randomTicks()` and stop at 3; vanilla's bamboo stops between 12 and 16 with leaves none, small, large from the bottom; the big dripleaf tilts after 2, 10 and 10 ticks and stays fully tilted for about 100; a project cactus hurt a pig as vanilla's did. The cactus flower was not seen in 20,000 forced ticks (verify at implementation). **X4:** a pot built on `FlowerPotBlock` holding a vanilla plant takes over vanilla's empty pot for that plant, so a project pot is its own class with its own map and vanilla's pot is untouched.
- **D1 as built** (B506, B509, B511, B516). The format froze as the B506 notes list: `growing_plant` (`direction`, `max_age` 0..25 as the starting age, `growth_chance`, `bonemeal_growth { min, max }`, `needs_water`, `berries { item, light }` with light 14 by default, `climbable`, `soil` of 1 to 16 ids or `#tags`), `column_plant` (`style`, `max_height` 1..32 defaulting to 3 or 16, `min_height`, `soil`, `needs_water_nearby`, `touch_damage`), `multiface` (`light` 7, `spreads`, `waterloggable`) and `dripleaf` (`size`, `grows_into` required for small, `tilt_ticks` [2, 10, 10] for big). The runtime has `MsmGrowingPlantHeadBlock` and `BodyBlock` (water plants over `KelpBlock` and `KelpPlantBlock` in nested `Water` classes), `MsmColumnPlantBlock.Cane` and `.Cactus`, `MsmBambooBlock` and `MsmBambooShootBlock` (our own rules), `MsmMultifaceBlock`, and `MsmBigDripleafBlock`, `MsmDripleafStemBlock` and `MsmSmallDripleafBlock` (the small one placed by a double-high item), made by `registry/types/GrowingPlantTypes`. Companions as D4: `<path>_plant` (the body), `<path>_shoot` (bamboo only), `<path>_stem` (big dripleaf only), and for cave-vine berries the item `<id>_berries`; `companionsOf(type, data)` depends on the data. Block states: `berries` only when `berries` is set; `age` and `leaves` on bamboo; `facing` and `tilt` on the big leaf; `facing` and `half` on the small leaf. Project bamboo and its shoot join `#minecraft:supports_bamboo`. Validators `msm.blocks-items.plants-3` (`PLANT3_PROBLEM_CODES`: the dripleaf pairing, `column-min-above-max`, `water-plant-feature-dry` and the six B515 added). The Block Studio's "Plants" group has the four types with their sections and labels (Grows up/Grows down, Starts at age, Chance to grow, Lives in water, Berries, Can be climbed, Grows on, Tallest/Shortest stop, Needs water beside it, Hurts when touched, Spreads with bone meal, Big/Small leaf, Grows into).
- **Soil** (amendment, vetoable). A given `soil` list is the element's own `canSurvive` check; without one the plant uses vanilla's tags (`TargetFacts.blocks.plantSoils`, plugin API 1.19.0, identical on both targets). A cave-vine-like plant may join `#minecraft:cave_vines` for climbing and bee growth; a kelp-like plant refuses `#minecraft:cannot_support_kelp`.
- **Behaviour found while building** (B509): a block with `msm:on_interact` takes every right-click, bone meal included; bamboo's stop height is fixed per column and bone meal can pass it up to `max_height`; ripe berry light replaces `msm:light_emitter`, and a multiface's `light` gives way to it; with `waterloggable: false` the waterlogged property still exists but water is refused; cane and cactus heights other than 3 use the runtime's own random tick, because vanilla's cap is fixed. Strands drop only to shears or Silk Touch (or sometimes by luck); lichen-like plants and small dripleaf only to shears.
- **D2 as built** (B510, B510b, B512, B515). `MsmFlowerPotBlock` (a plain `Block`, never a `FlowerPotBlock`) is the empty pot and each plant in it; `PotTypes.afterBlockRegistered` registers one potted companion `<pot path>_<plant path>` per `plants` entry, after the pot or after a later project plant, with the log line "pot <id>: registered N potted plant(s) of M listed; K wait for their plant". Seven uses match vanilla's pot in survival and creative; putting a plant in counts as potting, taking it out does not. Lowering: the empty pot on `minecraft:block/flower_pot`, each potted companion on `flower_pot_cross` (the first plant of a path wins), the block tag `flower_pots`, potted loot dropping pot and plant; `potted_plant.pot` (default `minecraft:flower_pot`) puts a project plant into a project pot. A plant in a project pot is drawn from the plant's own block picture, so plants vanilla pots with a model of their own (cactus, bamboo, azalea) look flat.
- **D3** holds for the head and body plants, cane, cactus and lichen (vanilla's classes over our numbers); bamboo and dripleaf follow the amendment (our own rules from the measured numbers, checked by parity cases against vanilla).
- **D5 as built** (B507, B515). `BlockFacts.plantSoils` (`cane`, `caneWater`, `cactus`, `cactusFlower`, `bamboo`, `bigDripleaf`, `smallDripleaf`, `notKelp`, `caveVines`, tag ids without `#`); `modelTemplates` grew to 87 with 19 plant and pot templates.
- **Tests** (B509, B510, B520). GameTests per family on both versions; `SamplePlantsAndStorageParityTest` covers the sea ribbon, glow vine, climbing ivy, reed, cactus (against vanilla's damage), bamboo shoot, moss, wide leaves and the clay planter beside vanilla's pot; `MsmPlants3ClientGameTest` shows a strand in a glass tank, a ripe strand at block light 12, a cactus column and a project pot; twelve sim-parity scenarios recorded in the game agree with Try it (the berry strand's "nothing below" check was fixed by hanging it one block higher).
- **Open follow-ups.** The cactus flower (not seen); a wide-leaf tilt parity case; a `pot_textures` field so a `potted_plant` in a project pot shows the project pot's picture (the lowering sees one element); e2e for Desert plants, dripleaf and multiface in the Studio; client GameTest screenshots of every family on 26.4 beyond the row.

## Status

Accepted with findings (2026-11-22, close B523; unreleased, awaiting the user's in-game checks). The user has not yet been shown the defaults and may still veto them. Vetoable defaults as built: four plant types rather than one per vanilla plant; vanilla's numbers as defaults (a growing head 26 blocks at most, growth chance 0.14, sugar cane's and cactus's height of 3, bamboo's 16, glow lichen's light 7, berry light 14, cactus damage 1, the dripleaf's 2, 10 and 10 ticks); our own rules for bamboo and dripleaf over the measured numbers; a `soil` list checked by the plant itself rather than added to vanilla's tags; the companion names `<path>_plant`, `<path>_shoot`, `<path>_stem` and `<pot>_<plant>`; a pot lists its plants rather than taking every plant; a project pot its own class, never vanilla's pot. Proposed at first (2026-11-22) with these defaults: four plant types rather than one per vanilla plant; vanilla's numbers as defaults (kelp's age, sugar cane's and cactus's height of 3, bamboo's 16, glow lichen's light 7, cactus damage 1: all "verify at implementation"); the companion names `<path>_plant`, `<path>_shoot`, `<path>_stem` and `<pot>_<plant>`; a pot lists its plants rather than taking every plant.
