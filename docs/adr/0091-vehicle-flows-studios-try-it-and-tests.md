# ADR 0091: Vehicle flows, Studios, Try it and tests

## Context

ADRs [0088](0088-client-channel-and-key-binds.md), [0089](0089-hud-elements.md) and [0090](0090-vehicles.md) add three kinds (`msm:key_bind`, `msm:hud`, `msm:vehicle`), the runtime's first networking channel and its first client input. Earlier batches paired their format ADRs with one ADR for the app, the library and the tests ([ADR 0073](0073-mob-flows-templates-and-presets.md), [ADR 0078](0078-portal-flows-studio-and-tests.md), [ADR 0081](0081-particle-studio-flows-and-tests.md)); this is that ADR for the "Vehicles and the client side" batch. The app's renderer bundle is at 15.0 of its 16 MB budget (HANDOFF), and HANDOFF asks for a decision on the renderer reserve before backlog B15 adds UI. Try it (ADR 0085) is in progress; its slice 2 draws mobs and particles and slice 3 runs plain events.

## Decision

- **D1 Studios.** A Vehicle Studio (registered through `studios/entities/studios.tsx`) with sections Model and parts (bone pickers read from the geometry: turret, barrel, wheels, steering, rotors, muzzles), Driving (mode picker with a picture per mode, speed in blocks a second shown beside blocks a tick), Seats (placed by dragging markers on the preview), Engine and fuel, Weapons, Turret, Health and repair, Storage, Paint, Sounds, Camera, Item and the Rules section (ADR 0082). The live preview reuses the geo preview: the turret follows the mouse, wheels and rotors spin with a "Drive" slider, the muzzle and seats show as markers. A Key bind Studio (default key by "Press a key", conflicts with vanilla controls named) and a HUD Studio (ADR 0089 D9's layout preview) are registered through a new `studios/controls/studios.tsx`.
- **D2 Creator flows.** Four flows, each a library starter with `creator` (ADR 0043), each making one vehicle element, its item recipe (optional), textures painted from a pick of colours, a geometry from a bundled MSM-made model (our own boxes, no Mojang models), sounds chosen from vanilla sound events and, where it has weapons, a throwable shell item: **Car** (ground, two seats, horn, fuel optional), **Tank** (ground, turns in place, turret and cannon, explosive shell, ammunition optional, repaired with iron ingots, health 120), **Plane** (air, one or two seats, fuel), **Boat** (water, two to four seats, a paddle or engine sound). A fifth, **Helicopter** (hover), is a template, not a flow (D3). Each flow's untouched defaults create a project with zero problems (the block types batch's lesson), proven in e2e.
- **D3 Templates and examples.** Templates in the entities plugin (category "Vehicles"): Go-kart, Car, Tank, Biplane, Helicopter, Motorboat, Raft, Hovercraft (amphibious). Templates in the controls plugin (category "Controls and HUD"): Dash key, Ability key with cooldown, Mana bar, Stamina bar, Coordinates text, Score counter. Library examples (golden in `examples/`): "Vehicles: a tank battle" (tank, shell, ammunition crate, a HUD reload text), "Vehicles: a seaside" (motorboat, raft, a dock block), "Controls: a dash ability" (key bind, a stamina bar, a Rules book with a cooldown counter). Mods the research surveyed (Tanks, Immersive Aircraft, Automobility, Small Ships) inspire the choices; nothing is copied from them.
- **D4 Very easy.** Home gains one tile, **Make a vehicle** ("Cars, tanks, planes and boats you can drive."), which asks "What kind?" with four pictures (Car, Tank, Plane, Boat), then the name, then runs the matching flow with its defaults. Key binds and HUD elements have no tiles: they appear only in Rules, as "When the player presses a key" (the key is captured by pressing it; the key bind element is made for the user) and "Show a number on screen". No ids are shown.
- **D5 Renderer budget.** B407 measures the renderer and picks one of two pre-approved routes before any UI brief starts: (a) load the Vehicle, Key bind and HUD Studios as lazy chunks that the budget counts separately (if `tools/check-size` can already tell lazy chunks apart), or (b) raise the renderer budget to 18 MB in `tools/check-size` with the measured reason recorded in this ADR's findings. A third route needs an orchestrator decision.
- **D6 Try it.** Vehicles appear in Try it (ADR 0085) at the "rough" fidelity level: the vehicle item places the vehicle, "Get in" seats the camera, and `@msm/sim` drives it with a TypeScript twin of `VehiclePhysics` tied to `fixtures/vehicle-physics/golden.json` (ADR 0090 D3), against the area's blocks with the simulation's own collision; the turret follows the camera, wheels and rotors turn, fuel burns down. Firing logs "Only in Minecraft: fires <projectile>" and plays nothing; ammunition, damage, storage, paint and passengers are shown as chips. Key binds pressed in Try it run their plain events through the simulation's trigger engine (ADR 0085 slice 3), and HUD elements whose source is `set`, `data` (Rules counters) or `vehicle` are drawn over the view by a small HTML layer using the same anchor rules; `score` and `player` sources show "Only in Minecraft". The permanent strip stays. This lands after the Try it batch's slice 3 is merged (its trigger engine) and is optional for this batch's close: if Try it has not reached slice 3, B428 and B429 move to the Try it plan.
- **D7 Tests.**
  - JUnit: the parsers of the three kinds over a conformance corpus (prefixes `key-bind-`, `hud-`, `vehicle-`), `VehiclePhysics` and its golden, `HudLayout`, the key guard (rate limit, unknown ids, held-set bounds).
  - GameTests (server): `MsmKeyBindGameTest` (payloads fed to the receiver for mock players: events, `when`, cooldown, hold interval, releases cleared on death and leaving), `MsmHudGameTest` (value computation and change detection, actions), `MsmVehicleGameTest` (placing, picking up with state, seats, fuel, damage and destroy, storage, server-side movement without a driver, `explode`), `MsmVehicleWeaponGameTest` (cooldown, ammunition, projectile owner, turret limits, seats' weapons), `MsmScriptApi15GameTest`.
  - Client GameTests on both versions, headless (ADR 0081 D6): `MsmControlsClientGameTest` connects to a real dedicated server (`createServer`, `connect`), holds a project key with `TestInput.holdKey` and checks the server-side effect, and checks a HUD bar with a screenshot and `assertScreenshotContains`; `MsmVehicleClientGameTest` places a vehicle, mounts it, holds forward and checks that the server sees it move and turn, fires with the fire key and checks the projectile, and screenshots the vehicle and dashboard.
  - Parity: `fixtures/sample-vehicles` (a tank, a car, a plane, a boat, a helicopter, key binds and HUD elements, a Rules book) with `SampleVehiclesParityTest` on the content jars of both versions (no ejected run: vehicles are not ejected).
  - e2e: `vehicles-path` (the Tank flow with defaults, the Vehicle Studio, export with zero problems, the Very easy tile) and `controls-path` (Key bind and HUD Studios, a Rules key trigger and HUD step).
  - CI: export, server-check (pinning `[msm_runtime] registered N vehicle type(s)`, `N key bind(s)`, `N HUD element(s)` and `--forbid-line NoClassDefFoundError`) and parity-run the sample vehicles on every version; the client GameTests run in the existing client step.
- **D8 AI helper.** The system prompt gains short paragraphs on vehicles, key binds and the HUD (prefer the flows' shapes; a fire key is a vehicle weapon, not a key bind; show values with HUD elements, not the action bar); `kind_schema` summaries for the three kinds stay within their allowances; a scripted build scenario "a tank that fires shells" passes `run_checks` with zero problems.
- **D9 Guide.** New pages `vehicles.md` and `keys-and-hud.md` (with the limits of this batch in plain words), lines in `rules.md`, `very-easy.md`, `playing-and-testing.md`, `scripting.md`, `try-it.md` (if D6 lands) and the site sidebar.

## Consequences

- The batch ends with the same proof as earlier ones: a sample fixture, parity tests on both versions, client GameTests for the new client code, e2e paths and a guide.
- The client GameTest route grows from "something is drawn" to real input against a dedicated server.
- Very easy gains its fourteenth tile.

## Status

Proposed (2026-11-14). Defaults the user may veto: the Studio sections and the drag-to-place seats (D1); four Creator flows (Car, Tank, Plane, Boat) and Helicopter as a template only, MSM-made models (D2); the template and example names (D3); one Very easy tile "Make a vehicle" with its wording, and keys and HUD only through Rules there (D4); the two renderer budget routes (D5); vehicles in Try it as "rough" with firing logged only, and moving it to the Try it plan if that batch is not ready (D6); no ejected parity run (D7).
