# ADR 0078: Portal flows, Studio section, templates and tests

## Context

[ADR 0077](0077-custom-portal-blocks.md) adds the `portal` block type, the `light_portal` action and the portal index. Beginners need a portal in one Create, next to the Dimension flow of [ADR 0051](0051-dimension-creator-flow.md); power users need every field in the Block Studio; the library needs templates and an example; and the runtime behaviour needs GameTests, black-box parity tests on both targets and on the ejected jar, and an in-game checklist for what CI cannot see (the overlay, the wobble, the sounds, particles; audit L16). `app.asar` is 31.5 of its 32 MB budget (audit L15), so new UI is loaded on demand and new library content stays small; the reserve brief B329 (vendored target data outside the asar) runs first if any brief reports more than 31.8 MB. The facts and limits (P1–P18) are in ADR 0077's Findings from implementation.

## Decision

- **D1 Block Studio: a "Portal" section.** Shown only for `type: portal`, loaded with `React.lazy` from `studios/blocks-items/PortalSection.tsx` (model in `portal-model.ts`), in three groups: **Frame** (frame block or block tag picker, "Exit portals are built of" block picker shown when the frame is a tag, "Light it with" item or item tag picker with "Nothing: a trigger lights it"), **Travel** (destination and "Comes back to" dimension pickers listing project dimensions and the target's vanilla dimensions, "Scale" as "From the dimensions" or a number with an "8:1 like the Nether" shortcut, "Arrive" as "In a linked portal" or "On the ground", delay, cooldown, "Players only", "Wobble and overlay") and **Looks** (particle type, "Colour" for dust, particles per tick, ambient and travel sound pickers with "None"). `on_travel` is edited in the existing events editor; the Textures section gains the slot `portal` ("Portal surface"); the type preview draws the pane model for both axes; the events editor's action picker gains "Light a portal" with a project portal picker. Every change is one undoable command.
- **D2 Creator flows** (group `worlds`, starters with `creator` under the rules of [ADR 0043](0043-creator-guided-flows.md) and the `meta.creator` reopen rule):
  - **"Portal"** (card blurb "Portal to a dimension"): name; **Where it goes** (a new dimension, the default, with the Dimension flow's Terrain, Biome and Sky questions; the Nether; the End; or another dimension by id); **Frame** (a new frame block in the portal colour, the default, or crying obsidian, gold block, quartz block, prismarine bricks or purpur block); **Light it with** (flint and steel, the default; a new key item with 64 uses; a fire charge; an ender eye); **Scale** (1:1, the default, or 8:1 like the Nether); **Portal colour** (tints the portal and frame placeholders and the dust particles); **Particles** (portal sparkles, the default, or coloured dust); **Sounds** (the portal hum, the default, or quiet); **Wobble and overlay when entering** (on). With a new dimension it also writes the Dimension flow's `<id>/travel` function, so a vanilla data pack still has a way in.
  - **The Dimension flow** gains a third answer for **How to get there**: "A portal (needs the Fabric mod jar)", which adds the Portal flow's portal, frame block and lang with its default answers and the dimension's colour.
  Both need the Fabric mod jar for the portal (`supports: hasFabric`), create with zero problems on both targets with untouched defaults, and reopen with every answer kept.
- **D3 Templates** (`@msm/plugin-blocks-items`, category "Portals", vanilla ids only): "Portal to the Nether" (crying obsidian, 8:1 from the dimension types, coloured dust), "Portal to the End" (end stone bricks, `arrival: surface`), "Trigger-lit portal" (`igniter: false`, quartz frame, to the Nether; its description shows the `light_portal` action). Three templates.
- **D4 Example** (`@msm/plugin-library`): "Two-way sky portal": a flat sky dimension with a vanilla dimension type, a portal with a project frame block, a block whose `msm:on_interact` runs `light_portal`, and an `on_travel` function that says a line. It passes the library test on both targets and its vanilla data pack loads on vanilla servers (the portal's tag entry made optional, the frame block's loot table left out).
- **D5 AI helper.** The system prompt's block knowledge gains the `portal` type, its fields and defaults, `light_portal`, `on_travel` and the limits users meet (mod jar only, the Nether's overlay, vertical frames only, lit by the igniter or the action only); element summaries name the destination and the frame.
- **D6 Testing.** Runtime GameTests in three new classes (`MsmPortalFrameGameTest`, `MsmPortalBlockGameTest`, `MsmPortalTravelGameTest`) on 26.3, 26.3 without GeckoLib and 26.4; a new fixture `fixtures/sample-portals` (rather than more of `sample-mod`, whose goldens and 83 parity tests stay as they are) with a project dimension, a portal to it with a project frame block, a portal to the Nether with a tag frame, a project key igniter and dust particles, a surface-arrival portal for players only, a trigger block lighting a portal with no igniter, and `on_travel` functions that say a line; its content jar is server-checked on both targets (the log pins `registered N portal block(s)`), and about fourteen black-box parity tests (`SamplePortalsParityTest`: frames built with `/fill`, lit through `ServerPlayerGameMode.useItemOn` by a mock player, travel by an item entity or a mob, arrival dimension and position read back) run on the 26.3 content jar, the 26.3 ejected jar and the 26.4 content jar; an e2e path `portals-path` (both flows with defaults, one edit per Portal field kept on reopen, export on both targets, the vanilla data pack warnings). The overlay, wobble, entry sound, particles, ambient sound and the feel of the trip are in the fixture's README checklist for the user's in-game check.
- **D7 Guide.** A new page `docs/guide/portals.md` (with a sidebar entry), linked from `worldgen.md`, `block-types-and-plants.md` and `creator.md`, saying what players see and the limits users meet (P1, P2, P3, P5, P6, P9 and P10 of ADR 0077's findings).

## Consequences

- A beginner gets a dimension and a lit-able portal in one Create; a Dimension flow user gets the portal by one more answer; existing Dimension flow projects are unchanged until reopened and switched.
- The Block Studio grows by one lazy chunk; the library by one flow, three templates, one example and two placeholder patterns (portal surface, frame), checked against the asar budget in every app and library brief.
- The in-game checklist is the only check of the client effects; CI checks blocks, links, positions and dimensions.

## Findings from implementation

Recorded at close (2026-11-14) from B335, B337, B338, B340, B341, B342, B343 and B344; the fact table and limits are in [ADR 0077](0077-custom-portal-blocks.md) Findings from implementation.

- **Portal section** (D1). Section "Portal" (test id `block-portal`, a lazy chunk of about 9 kB) with the groups Frame, Travel and Looks and the labels of D1 ("Frame block or #tag", "Exit portals are built of", "Light it with (item or #tag)", "Nothing: a trigger lights it", "Destination", "Comes back to", "Scale" as "From the dimensions" or "A number" with "8:1 like the Nether", "Arrive" as "In a linked portal" or "On the ground", "Players only", "Wobble and overlay", "On travel"); the texture slot "Portal surface"; `portal` is the 15th type in the Type picker and Models are hidden for it. Switching to `portal` keeps only the allowed components, drops a default `item` (and then `loot: "self"`) and writes `frame: minecraft:crying_obsidian` and `destination: minecraft:the_nether`; switching away drops the nine portal fields. "Light a portal (light_portal)" is offered by the events editors of the Block, Item, Effect and Entity Studios, with a picker of the project's portals.
- **Flows** (D2). The Portal flow is in group `worlds` after Dimension, `supports: hasFabric`, with groups Portal, Where it goes, Frame, Travel and Looks (labels in the source: "New dimension name", "Frame", "Light it with", "Portal colour"); its defaults make `demo:sky_realm`, `demo:sky_realm/travel`, `demo:sky_portal`, `demo:sky_portal_frame` and the recipe `demo:sky_portal_frame_from_obsidian`. The Dimension flow's answer is "A portal (needs the Fabric mod jar)" (ids `<dim>_portal`, `<dim>_portal_frame`) and its colour question is now "Key or portal colour". A flow never puts the project frame block in a flat layer or feature (it would leave the dimension out of vanilla packs). Two new placeholder patterns, `portal` (translucent) and `portal_frame`: 66 patterns, 45 starters (33 Creator flows).
- **Templates and example** (D3, D4). Template ids `msm_block_portal_nether`, `msm_block_portal_end` and `msm_block_portal_trigger_lit` (category "Portals"; blocks-items 56, built-ins 188). The example `example_msm_sky_portal` ("Two-way sky portal": `sky_isle`, `sky_gate` with dust and `on_travel` running `sky_gate/arrived`, `sky_gate_beacon` running `light_portal`); 129 examples. The library vanilla server check passes on both targets.
- **AI helper** (D5, changed). A "Portals" paragraph after "Containers and machines" (about 2.7 kB, 660 tokens, both modes); the per-element summary is dropped, as in ADR 0076, because `project_summary` only counts kinds. The AI tools' placeholder pattern list gains `portal` and `portal_frame`, and `list_starters` names Storage crate, Machine and Portal.
- **Testing** (D6, changed). Thirteen parity tests, not about fourteen, in `SamplePortalsParityTest` (filter `msm_parity_tests:sample_portals_*`), each passing on the 26.3 content jar, the 26.3 ejected jar and the 26.4 content jar. Because a data pack dimension makes no level on the GameTest server (ADR 0077 F20, limit P8), no parity test travels into `sky_realm`: travel is checked on `ember_portal` to the Nether (x and z divided by 8, one 2 by 3 exit of gold on the entry's axis, the way back through `Portal.getPortalDestination`), and `sky_portal` and `drift_portal` are checked to take nobody; the content run allows only that missing-level warning (`tools/run-fabric-server --allow-line`), while the server checks forbid it (`--forbid-line`). The `on_travel` line is not parity-tested: only `sky_portal` has one. The fixture `fixtures/sample-portals` (namespace `msm_sample_portals`) has `sky_realm`, `sky_stone`, `sky_portal`, the tag `ember_frame` (gold, copper, waxed copper), `ember_key` (64 uses), `ember_portal` (dust 14848032, count 6, ambient `msm_sample_portals:ember.hum`), `drift_portal` and `drift_beacon`. The e2e spec `portals-path` has two tests (22 e2e specs, 43 tests in all).
- **Asar.** After B337 and B338 `app.asar` was 31.7 MB, just under the 31.8 MB stop line, so the reserve brief B329 ran in this batch ([ADR 0079](0079-compact-vendored-target-data-in-the-asar.md)); after the batch it is 26.9 MB (28,182,916 bytes, at b605da4).

## Status

Accepted (2026-11-14), with the defaults below; the user may still veto them. Vetoable defaults: the section name "Portal" and its labels (D1); the flow name "Portal", its card blurb, its questions, choices and defaults (a new dimension, a new frame block, flint and steel, 1:1, portal sparkles, the portal hum, the overlay on) and the key item's 64 uses (D2); the Dimension flow's third travel answer (D2); writing the travel function as well when the Portal flow makes a new dimension (D2); the three template names and their choices (D3); the example (D4); a new fixture rather than more of the sample mod (D6).
