# ADR 0086: Surprise me: a random mod

## Context

Testing 1.5.1 on Windows, the user looked at Very easy Home ([ADR 0083](0083-very-easy-mode.md)) and asked for "a randomise a mod option if someone is not sure what to do" and whether "more things will be added here". Home's 13 tiles reached most Creator flows ([ADR 0043](0043-creator-guided-flows.md)) but not the Painting, Villager trade, Extra drop or Scripted behaviour flows, and none of the library starters that are not flows (the custom biome, the jigsaw hamlet, the recipe pack, the resource pack). A beginner who does not know what to make has no starting point at all.

Options for the random mod: (a) a fixed list of hand-written example mods picked at random, which never surprises twice and goes stale as flows change; (b) the AI helper, which needs a key and is not deterministic; (c) a seeded generator that answers the existing Creator flows' own questions. We take (c): it reuses everything the Creator already validates, every set it makes reopens in the Creator, and it adapts when a flow gains a question.

## Decision

- **D1 What it makes.** A small themed mod: one **material** (the Custom ore flow, or the Gear set flow with its ore) and two to four **extras** from different slots: a building block (Simple block or Stone set), a weapon, a food (Food or Consumable), a creature (Custom mob, Pet, Farm animal or Flying mob), magic (Potion or Status effect), a plant (Crop or Tree), sparkles (Particle) or a portal (Portal to a new dimension). Slots are weighted (portals and sparkles are rarer). With the rules kind available, about six in ten surprises also get one rule book: "when a player breaks the ore, N times in 100 an extra gem drops" (`<namespace>:<theme>_luck`). The Wood set (fifteen blocks) is left out to keep a surprise small.
- **D2 Themes, names, colours, answers.** Ten themes (Ruby, Frost, Ember, Moss, Sky, Void, Sun, Coral, Amber, Storm), each a word and four colours. Each flow's recipe (`app/src/renderer/src/surprise/themes.ts`) names its name answers from the theme word and a noun list (a weapon's noun follows its type: "Ruby Bow", "Ruby Shield"), pins a few answers (the material's ore generates; a portal leads to a new world) and avoids a few (a mob that spawns nowhere). Every other answer comes from the flow's own parameter list: a number near its default (within half the distance to `min` and `max`) on its steps, any choice, most of a set's default values and a few others, the theme's colours (the first colour question gets the main colour), no picture or sound, so the flow draws its tinted placeholder. A `x_min`/`x_max` pair is put in order. Answers always pass the wizard's own checks.
- **D3 Seeded and shown.** A seed is a whole number from 1 to 999 999 (mulberry32). The theme comes from its own stream of the seed, so "Change theme" keeps the same things with new names and colours. The dialog shows the seed in a field: the same seed, theme, project and target give the same mod, and typing a seed shows that seed's mod.
- **D4 Preview first.** The **Surprise me** dialog lists the mod's name ("Ruby Mod"), each thing with up to three of its pictures (item, block and first particle frame textures from the bundle), what it is in words and what it makes ("Makes Ruby Ore, Block of Ruby and Ruby."), and the rule in a sentence, with **Make it**, **Roll again**, **Change theme** (a row of theme chips) and **Cancel**. Nothing is written before **Make it**.
- **D5 One undo step; open or new mod.** Things are planned one after another with the Creator's own `planCreate` against the project as it would be after the earlier things, and every change goes in through `applyChangeset` as one undo step, "Surprise me: Ruby Mod". Each set carries `meta.creator`, so My things shows it and **Edit in Creator** reopens it. With a mod open the things are added to it; a thing whose names clash with what the project (or an earlier thing) already has tries the recipe's other nouns and then numbered names ("Ruby Bricks 2"); shared files (pack and mod settings, language files, tags, and an id the same flow made for another set, such as the Custom ore flow's advancement root) are not clashes. With no mod open, **Make it** starts a new mod named after the theme ("Ruby Mod", then "Ruby Mod 2" when that folder is taken) in the default folder of [ADR 0083](0083-very-easy-mode.md) D6, then rolls the same seed and theme for it.
- **D6 Validity.** Only flows the target supports and whose kinds are registered are used. A test rolls 200 seeds on each built-in target, applies each mod, builds the Fabric content jar with no errors and no session errors, and undoes it to the project it started from.
- **D7 Pick for me.** The Creator gains **Pick for me** beside a flow's questions (not when editing a set): the same answers for that flow from a random seed, with a theme's names when the flow has a recipe.
- **D8 More Home tiles.** Home's tiles are grouped under headings: **Start here** (Surprise me, wide), **Blocks and items**, **Creatures**, **Magic**, **Worlds**, **Looks and sounds** and **Rules and help**, 24 tiles in all. New tiles: **Surprise me**; **Make a chest or machine** (Storage crate, Machine, moved out of Make a block); **Make a recipe** (the Recipe pack starter); **Make sparkles** (Particle, moved out of Make a potion or effect); **Make a magic wand** (Scripted behaviour); **Make a world or biome** (Dimension, and the Custom biome starter); **Make a building** (the jigsaw hamlet starter); **Change drops and trades** (Extra drop, Villager trade); **Change how Minecraft looks** (Mob skin, and the Resource pack starter); **Make a painting** (Painting); **Make a sound or music** (Music disc and Goat horn, moved out of Make an item). Every Creator flow is reached by a tile. A tile that names library starters as well as flows opens a small chooser: a flow card opens the Creator, a starter card adds the starter to the mod at once through `addBundle` as one undo step. A tile is hidden when none of its flows and starters is available on the target, as before.
- **D9 Entry points.** Home's **Surprise me** tile, a **Surprise me** button on the no-mod Home, File > **Surprise Me…** (menu command `surpriseMe`) and the palette's "Surprise me: make a random mod", in every mode.

## Consequences

- The generator is a pure, seeded module (`app/src/renderer/src/surprise/surprise.ts`) built on the Creator's planning; it adapts to new questions in a flow without change, and a new flow joins surprises with one recipe entry.
- Bundles of one change set are folded so each element has one item (an update of an element made earlier in the same set becomes part of its create): the session replays a changeset's commands, and a command cannot change an element it created itself.
- Sets from Dimension, Villager trade, Extra drop and the starter tiles have no block, item or mob, so My things (ADR 0083 D2) shows no card for them; a message says what was added.
- The Home tile list, order and words change; ADR 0083's tile table is replaced by D8.

## Findings at close (B403)

Reviewed 2026-11-21 at the close of the Try it plan (B403), against the code at dac9ae5 (merged as 126b4e4, "Surprise me and more Home tiles"): `app/src/renderer/src/surprise/` (`themes.ts`, `surprise.ts`), `components/surprise/SurpriseDialog.tsx`, `components/home/tiles.ts`, `MakeTiles.tsx`, `MakeChooser.tsx`, the Creator's **Pick for me**, `main/menu.ts` and the palette; tests `app/test/surprise.test.ts` (200 seeds by default, `SURPRISE_SEEDS` overrides), `test/components/surprise-dialog.test.tsx`, `Home.test.tsx` and e2e `surprise-path`. The code matches D1 to D9; the ADR is accepted.

- **D1, D2 as built.** Ten themes of a word and four colours (`SURPRISE_THEMES`); 17 flow recipes in `FLOW_RECIPES` (material: Custom ore, Gear set; building: Simple block, Stone set; weapon; food: Food, Consumable; creature: Custom mob, Pet, Farm animal, Flying mob; magic: Potion, Status effect; plant: Crop, Tree; sparkles: Particle; portal: Portal); `SLOT_WEIGHTS` building, weapon, food and creature 3, magic and plant 2, sparkles and portal 1. The lucky-ore rule book comes with a chance of 0.6 when `msm:rules` is registered (`<namespace>:<theme>_luck`, then `_luck_<n>`).
- **D3, D5 as built.** mulberry32, `MAX_SEED` 999 999, the theme from its own stream. **Detail beyond the ADR:** set ids come from `crypto.randomUUID` in the app (`newSetId`), because two surprises of one seed in one mod otherwise shared set ids and My things merged their cards; `foldItems` merges an update of an element made earlier in the same change set into its create, because a command cannot change an element it created itself.
- **D6 as built.** The test rolls 200 seeds on mc-26.3 and mc-26.4, applies each, builds the content jar with no errors and no session errors and undoes it; every flow's answers pass `validateParameter`; a second surprise into the same mod avoids clashes; tile starters validate on both targets.
- **D8 differs in count, not in rule.** Home now has 27 tiles under eight headings: the later batches added **Vehicles** (heading and "Make a vehicle", ADR 0091), "Make your mod do something" (commands and creative tabs) and "Make a trader" (both ADR 0101 D4), and the headings keep D8's order otherwise. Every Creator flow is still reached by a tile, and a tile with library starters opens `MakeChooser`. DESIGN §8's Very easy paragraph now describes the grouped tiles.
- **D9 as built.** Home's tile and the no-mod button, File > **Surprise Me…** (`surpriseMe`), the palette's "Surprise me: make a random mod". The dialog also carries the **AI pictures** switch of [ADR 0093](0093-ai-pictures.md) (`surprise-ai-pictures`), off unless an image service is set.
- **Open follow-ups.** My things shows no card for sets without a block, item or mob (Dimension, Villager trade, Extra drop) or for starter tiles' content; `ProjectSession.applyChangeset` cannot change an element created earlier in the same change set (worked around by `foldItems`); recipes for the flows of later batches (a vehicle, a trader, a fluid, the twenty block types); Surprise me's pictures become drawn ones with the art batch (B497, `docs/PLAN-art.md`).

## Status

Proposed (2026-11-14), implemented on branch `surprise` and merged (126b4e4); **accepted with findings at close B403 (2026-11-21)**, unreleased until the 1.5.2 draft. Vetoable defaults: the ten themes and their words; the slots, their weights and the flows in them; two to four extras; the lucky-ore rule and its chance; seeds up to 999 999; the new mod's name "<Theme> Mod"; Pick for me in the Creator; the tile groups, labels and order; starter tiles adding at once rather than through the Examples gallery.
