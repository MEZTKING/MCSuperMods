# ADR 0110: Boats and chest boats for project woods

## Context

Every vanilla wood has a boat and a chest boat (bamboo a raft). The Wood set flow ([ADR 0069](0069-block-family-flows.md)) makes logs, planks, slabs, stairs, doors, signs and the rest, but no boat; the coverage audit lists boats under "client, L3" because each boat entity type needs a renderer. The vehicles batch ([ADR 0090](0090-vehicles.md)) has its own vehicle kind with its own renderer, which is not a vanilla boat (no paddles, no vanilla handling). Vanilla draws boats with one renderer class per entity type over a model layer and a texture named after the wood; registering that renderer for a project boat may be a line of client registration rather than rendering work of our own (spike question X24).

## Decision

- **D1 Boats as an item component.** `msm:item` gains `msm:boat { chest?: boolean, raft?: boolean, wood_texture? }`: the runtime registers the entity type `<path>` (and with `chest` the companion item and type `<path>_chest_boat`; limit N2), vanilla's boat or chest boat behaviour (paddling, water and ice handling, two seats, the chest boat's menu), and the item that places it. The Wood set flow's "Has boats" answer adds both items, their recipes as vanilla's, the `#minecraft:boats` and `#minecraft:chest_boats` tags and the entity textures drawn by the art generator.
- **D2 Drawing.** The client registers vanilla's boat renderer for each project boat type through the `EntityRendererRegistration` seam with the project texture. No renderer of our own.
- **D3 Gate.** If the spike (B524) finds that vanilla's renderer cannot draw a project boat with only that registration on either version, boats move to the next client batch (reserve B545): the component stays unoffered and the guide says so (limit N19).
- **D4 Fabric mod jar only**; vanilla packs leave the items out (ADR 0066).

## Consequences

- Wood sets become complete with boats on both versions, if X24 allows.
- The runtime gains its first use of a vanilla entity renderer for a project type.

## Amendment after the spike (B524, 2026-11-22)

**Go on both versions (B524, X24; evidence in `docs/reference/spikes/b524/`).** Vanilla's `Boat` and `ChestBoat` with a `BoatItem`, the oak boat's size (1.375 x 0.5625, eye 0.5625, tracking 10), and on the client a model layer `<ns>:boat/<path>#main` (and `chest_boat/<path>`) registered through Fabric's `ModelLayerRegistry` with vanilla's `BoatRenderer`, draw and ride a project boat exactly as vanilla's; textures `<ns>:textures/entity/boat/<path>.png` (128 x 64) and `chest_boat/<path>.png` (128 x 128), drawn by the art generator. No version seam. B536 runs; reserve B545 is not needed. Dispenser behaviour is to check at implementation.

## Findings at close (B544, 2026-11-22)

Recorded at the close of vanilla parity 3 batch 2 (B544) from the code on the session branch at a57dbd3 and the plan's notes: B524 (a11bfed, the spike; amendment c473792), B526 (a8a416b), B527 (04d9019), B531 (c13fcf5), B536 (cc49b1d), B537b (2bf7321), B538 (054e871), B539 (851ff8e), B540 (074e78e), B541 (f565cf9) and B543 (66043fb). In the 1.5.3 test build; awaiting the user's in-game checks (`fixtures/sample-mobs-and-commands/README.md`).

- **Spike answer X24** (B524, both versions; `docs/reference/spikes/b524/`): go. Our `Boat` and `ChestBoat` entity types with `BoatItem`s, Fabric's `ModelLayerRegistry` with vanilla's layer definitions and vanilla's `BoatRenderer` through the `EntityRendererRegistration` seam draw a project texture (`<ns>:textures/entity/boat/<path>.png`, `chest_boat/<path>.png`); placed, ridden, paddled, the chest boat's 27-slot menu and the drops on breaking all worked. D3's gate is passed: **B536 ran and reserve B545 was not needed**; limit N19's "possibly the client batch" is lifted.
- **D1 as built** (B527, B531, B536). Item component `msm:boat { chest?, raft?, wood_texture? }` (element-only pictures beside it). An item with it is vanilla's boat under the item's own id: `registry/boat/MsmBoats.itemFor` (called by `MsmRegistrar` in place of a plain item) registers the entity type `<item id>` as vanilla's `Boat` (or `Raft` with `raft`) and returns `MsmBoatItem`, a `BoatItem` on which item events still fire. No boat class of our own exists: paddling, water and ice, the two seats, the chest menu and the drops are vanilla's. **Changed from D1:** the chest companion is `<wood>_chest_boat` (or `<wood>_chest_raft`), where `<wood>` is the item's path without its `_boat` or `_raft` ending (vanilla's naming), not `<path>_chest_boat`; it is a plain `BoatItem` with no definition, rarity or events of its own, and sits in the boat's creative tab right after it. Sizes, tracking range and update interval are read from vanilla's oak boat, oak chest boat, bamboo raft and bamboo chest raft at registration. Both items get vanilla's boat dispenser behaviour (tested). An entity type id that is taken fails the boat item; a taken chest boat id is an error and the boat comes without its chest boat.
- **Lowering** (B531). Boat items stack to 1 and join `#minecraft:boats`; the chest companion joins `#minecraft:chest_boats`; the recipes are vanilla's shapes (five `<wood>_planks` in a U; the boat and a chest, shapeless). The planks are named after the item, not after `wood_texture`, so borrowing vanilla oak's pictures never makes a second oak boat recipe; missing planks are a warning on the boat. Entity pictures sit at vanilla's layout (`boatTexturePaths`: 128 x 64 and 128 x 128). Validators `msm.blocks-items.boats` (`BOAT_PROBLEM_CODES`, 6 codes).
- **D2 as built.** `client/boat/BoatRenderers` registers one model layer per wood, `<wood ns>:boat/<wood path>#main` and `chest_boat/...` (reusing a layer vanilla already has for a vanilla `wood_texture`), with vanilla's `BoatRenderer` or `RaftRenderer`. A boat and a raft of the same wood share one layer: the first decides, with a warning. No renderer of our own and no version seam beyond `EntityRendererRegistration`.
- **D4 as built.** Vanilla packs leave out the boat items, their recipes and tag entries (`chestBoatIdOf` lists the companion as mod-only). The 26.3 eject writes boats (`boat/ModBoats`, client `BoatRenderers`; B537b).
- **The rest of the product** (ADR 0112 findings): the Item Studio's **Boat** section (`BoatSection.tsx`; pictures copied to the game's paths in the same undo step), the Wood set flow's **Has boats** (off by default; boat and chest boat with drawn pictures, art recipes `boat`, `boat_layer`, `chest_boat_layer`), the template `msm_item_boat` and the example Willow boats, the guide page `boats.md`. Try it places boats as wood-coloured boxes on its water vehicles' physics.
- **Tests.** `MsmBoatGameTest` (5: placing, riding, the chest boat's menu, breaking, the dispenser) on both versions; the GameTest mod logs `registered 5 boat type(s)` and the client `boat renderers for N boat type(s) over M model layer(s)`; client screenshots of a project boat and chest boat; the parity case `theLarchBoatsArePlacedOnWaterAndFloatAsVanillasDo` on the content jars of both versions and the 26.3 ejected jar; the fixture's server check pins `2 boat type(s)`.
- **Found in the game.** A boat is placed only where its box touches no block (vanilla's rule, seen while B541 wrote the parity case). The guide says so.
- **Limits.** N1 (boats are fixed at start), N2 (the ids `<wood>_chest_boat` and `<wood>_chest_raft` are reserved) and N19 (the Fabric mod jar only) hold; N19's second half (boats possibly moving to the client batch) is lifted.
- **Open follow-ups.** In-game looks of the pictures on the boat from every side (limit N26); a raft's look beside a boat of the same wood; on 26.4 a boat slowing near the edge of a project water source (ADR 0108 X28).

## Status

Accepted with findings (2026-11-22, close B544; in the 1.5.3 test build, awaiting the user's in-game checks). The user has not yet been shown the defaults and may still veto them. Vetoable defaults as built: a component on items rather than a kind; vanilla's own boat, chest boat and raft classes with nothing overridden; the chest companion named `<wood>_chest_boat` after vanilla, without a definition, rarity or events of its own and only in the boat's tab; a boat and a raft of the same wood sharing one look; recipes from `<wood>_planks` named after the item; the Wood set's "Has boats" off by default and making both boats; rafts only when asked. Proposed at first (2026-11-22) with these defaults: a component on items rather than a kind; chest boats by default with the Wood set's "Has boats" answer; rafts only when asked.
