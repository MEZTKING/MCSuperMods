# ADR 0070: Mob bodies, movement modes and ranged attacks

## Context

`msm:entity` makes one kind of mob: a ground-walking `PathfinderMob` (`MsmEntity`) with six goals, attributes, triggers, sounds, a spawn egg, natural spawns and a GeckoLib model ([ADR 0028](0028-entity-definition-format-v1.md), [ADR 0057](0057-project-sound-events-and-mob-sounds.md), [ADR 0061](0061-more-triggers.md)). The coverage audit (L7, backlog B7) lists what is missing: ranged attacks, flying and swimming, taming, breeding and babies, riding, variants, boss bars, more mob kinds and a renderer without GeckoLib. This ADR covers the mob's base class, its movement and its fighting; taming, breeding, riding, variants and boss bars are [ADR 0071](0071-tameable-breedable-rideable-mobs-variants-and-boss-bars.md); the renderer without GeckoLib is [ADR 0072](0072-vanilla-model-renderer.md); flows, templates and library content are [ADR 0073](0073-mob-flows-templates-and-presets.md). The working plan (B282–B308) was deleted at close; its fact table and limits are kept under Findings below.

Facts read on 2026-11-14 with `javap` (JDK 25) over the Loom-cached merged jars `minecraft-merged-deobf-26.3.jar` and `minecraft-merged-deobf-26.4-snapshot-7.jar`, the cached Fabric API module jars and this repository (the fact table with every signature is under Findings, F1 to F18):

- **Vanilla behaviour hangs on the class.** Breeding, babies and taming live in `Animal`, `AgeableMob` and `TamableAnimal` (`isFood` and `getBreedOffspring` are abstract; `BreedGoal`, `FollowParentGoal`, `FollowOwnerGoal`, `SitWhenOrderedToGoal`, `OwnerHurtByTargetGoal` and `OwnerHurtTargetGoal` take an `Animal` or `TamableAnimal`). Hostility lives in `Monster` (`implements Enemy`; `isPreventingPlayerRest` stops sleeping nearby, `getSoundSource` is `HOSTILE`, peaceful removal). Java's single inheritance means one `MsmEntity extends PathfinderMob` cannot be all three.
- **Goals with class bounds.** `RangedBowAttackGoal<T extends Monster & RangedAttackMob>` and `RangedCrossbowAttackGoal<T extends Monster & RangedAttackMob & CrossbowAttackMob>` accept only monsters. `RangedAttackGoal(RangedAttackMob, double, int, float)` is the same on both versions, but its five-argument form takes `(int, int)` on 26.3 and `(IntSupplier, IntSupplier)` on 26.4.
- **Projectiles.** `ProjectileUtil.getMobArrow(LivingEntity, ItemStack, float, ItemStack)` makes a mob's arrow; `ProjectileItem.asProjectile(Level, Position, ItemStack, Direction)` plus `shoot` makes the projectile of every vanilla throwable item (`ArrowItem`, `SnowballItem`, `EggItem`, `ExperienceBottleItem`, `FireChargeItem`, `FireworkRocketItem`, `ThrowablePotionItem`, `TridentItem`, `WindChargeItem`; 26.4 adds `IceBallItem`). The runtime's own `MsmThrownItem(Level, LivingEntity, ItemStack)` ([ADR 0056](0056-throwables.md)) already takes any living thrower.
- **Movement controls.** `FlyingMoveControl(Mob, int maxTurn, boolean hoversInPlace)`, `SmoothSwimmingMoveControl(Mob, int, int, float, float, boolean)`, `SmoothSwimmingLookControl(Mob, int)`, `FlyingPathNavigation`, `WaterBoundPathNavigation` and `AmphibiousPathNavigation` `(Mob, Level)`, `WaterAvoidingRandomFlyingGoal(PathfinderMob, double)` and `RandomSwimmingGoal(PathfinderMob, double, int)` are identical on both versions; `Mob.moveControl`, `lookControl` and `navigation` are protected fields and `createNavigation(Level)` is called from the `Mob` constructor. `LivingEntity.travelFlying` (four floats on 26.3, three on 26.4), `checkFallDamage` and `jumpInLiquid` differ between versions; `moveRelative`, `move`, `causeFallDamage(double, float, DamageSource)`, `canBreatheUnderwater` and `getDefaultGravity` do not.
- **Spawn checks.** `Animal.checkAnimalSpawnRules` and `WaterAnimal.checkSurfaceWaterAnimalSpawnRules` are bound to their classes' entity types; `Monster.checkMonsterSpawnRules` takes any `Mob`. `MobCategory` has the same eight constants on both versions.
- **Fabric** `FabricEntityType.Builder.createMob(EntityFactory<T extends Mob>, MobCategory, UnaryOperator<Mob<T>>)` is identical in object-builder 24.1.13 (26.3) and 25.0.8 (26.4).
- **Not goal-driven.** Allays, axolotls, frogs, camels, sniffers, wardens, piglins and villagers run on `Brain` behaviours, not goals; phantoms, ghasts and vexes have their own move controls.

## Decision

- **D1 A `base` field picks the vanilla class.** The entity definition and element gain `base?: "mob" | "animal" | "monster"`. Absent means `mob`: today's `PathfinderMob`, so every existing definition registers and behaves exactly as before. `animal` builds on `TamableAnimal` (taming stays off unless `msm:tameable` is present, ADR 0071), which brings ages, breeding, leads and animal spawn checks; `monster` builds on `Monster`, which brings `Enemy` (iron golems attack it), no sleeping nearby, hostile sounds and removal in peaceful. We chose an explicit field over deriving the class from the components because the class changes vanilla behaviour beyond the components (wander preferences, despawning, golems), and a beginner sees it as the Studio's "Kind of mob" picker. Category and base stay separate fields (a `monster` base in the `creature` category is allowed, with a validator info).
- **D2 Runtime classes.** Three driver classes extend the vanilla bases: `MsmEntity extends PathfinderMob` (kept), `MsmAnimal extends TamableAnimal` and `MsmMonster extends Monster`, all implementing an `MsmMob` interface and delegating every shared behaviour to one `MobDriver` per entity built from the definition (goals, events, sounds, movement, ranged attacks, and the ADR 0071 modules), the same pattern as `BlockBehaviourDriver` ([ADR 0067](0067-block-types-and-states.md) D4). The GeckoLib bridge gets one subclass per base (`MsmGeoEntity`, `MsmGeoAnimal`, `MsmGeoMonster`), each a thin `GeoEntity` that delegates its controllers to one shared `GeoControllers` helper; they are still reached only through `GeckolibEntities` after `isModLoaded("geckolib")`. The registrar's maps become `EntityType<? extends Mob>`. Spawn checks of D6 that vanilla binds to a class are repeated in the runtime (as `any_light_monster` is today). Overrides whose signature changed between versions (`canBeABaby`, `setAgeLocked`, `travelFlying`, `checkFallDamage`, `jumpInLiquid`) are never overridden or called; `canBeABaby` and `setAgeLocked` are left alone, flight avoids fall damage through `causeFallDamage`.
- **D3 Movement modes.** `msm:movement` gains `mode?: "walk" | "fly" | "swim" | "amphibious"` (default `walk`), `flying_speed?: float` (the `flying_speed` attribute, default 0.4, a parrot's; a bee's is 0.6, corrected at close), `hovers?: boolean` (fly: stays in place when idle, as a bee; default true), `breathes_air?: boolean` (swim: needs to surface as a dolphin; default false), `dry_out_ticks?: int @ 20..` (swim without `breathes_air`: health is lost after this many ticks out of water, default 300 as vanilla's water animals) and `step_height?: float`. `fly`: `FlyingMoveControl` (max turn 20), `FlyingPathNavigation` that can open doors off and float on, no gravity, no fall damage; `wander` becomes `WaterAvoidingRandomFlyingGoal`. `swim`: `SmoothSwimmingMoveControl` and `SmoothSwimmingLookControl` with the dolphin's numbers (Findings), `WaterBoundPathNavigation`, breathes water, `travel` in water through `moveRelative` and `move` (both versions identical), `wander` becomes `RandomSwimmingGoal` (interval 40), the float goal is not added, and out of water it flops and dries out unless `breathes_air`. `amphibious`: `AmphibiousPathNavigation`, walks on land, swims in water with the walking move control, breathes both, `wander` stays the walking stroll (avoid water false). `swims` keeps its meaning (float goal) for `walk` and `fly`.
- **D4 `msm:ranged_attack` component** (like `msm:attack` for melee):

  ```
  struct RangedAttack {
    projectile: #[id="item"] string,     // minecraft:arrow, any vanilla projectile item (target fact), or a project item with msm:throwable
    damage?: float @ 0..,                 // arrows only: base damage, as setBaseDamage (hit ≈ damage x speed, rounded up); default 2
    speed?: float @ 0.1..5,               // launch speed in blocks per tick; default 1.6 (a skeleton's arrow)
    inaccuracy?: float @ 0..20,           // default 6
    interval_ticks?: int @ 5..,           // cooldown between shots; default 40
    charge_ticks?: int @ 0..200,          // aiming time before a shot (the bow pose); default 20
    range?: float @ 1..64,                // blocks; default 15
    sound?: #[id="sound_event"] string,   // default minecraft:entity.skeleton.shoot
    on_shoot?: string,                    // event after each shot; other = the target
  }
  ```

  Arrows (`minecraft:arrow`, `tipped_arrow`, `spectral_arrow`) go through `ProjectileUtil.getMobArrow` with a weapon stack of `minecraft:bow`, other vanilla projectile items through `ProjectileItem.asProjectile` and `shoot`, and a project item with `msm:throwable` through `MsmThrownItem`, so its damage, gravity and `on_hit_entity`/`on_hit_block` events apply with the mob as thrower. Every mob class implements `RangedAttackMob`. The list of vanilla projectile items is a target fact (D7). A projectile that is neither is a validator error and a runtime load error that drops the component.
- **D5 New goals.** `ranged_attack { speed?, strafe?: boolean }` is the runtime's own `MsmRangedAttackGoal` (approach until within `range` and in sight, set `aggressive` while charging so renderers can pose, shoot, wait `interval_ticks`; with `strafe` it circles as a skeleton), because vanilla's bow and crossbow goals accept only monsters and the plain ranged goal's interval form differs between versions; `ranged_attack` without `msm:ranged_attack` adds nothing (a validator warning, as `melee_attack` without `msm:attack`); its targets come from a `melee_attack`-style `targets`, `must_see` and `retaliate` on the goal. `tempt { items, speed?, scared?: boolean }` (`TemptGoal`), `panic { speed? }` (`PanicGoal`), `leap_at_target { height? }` (`LeapAtTargetGoal`). Goals of ADR 0071 (follow owner, sit, defend, breed, follow parent) are added by their components, not listed. Unknown goal types are still dropped with an error by older runtimes.
- **D6 Spawn rules and kinds.** `spawn.rule` gains `animal` (the animal check: grass-like ground and light, repeated by the runtime for any base) and `water_animal` (the surface water check). Categories are unchanged; the Studio's "Kind of mob" picker sets base, category, placement, rule and movement mode together (creature, farm animal, pet, monster, flying creature, water creature, fish, ambient), and every value stays editable.
- **D7 Versions.** All shared code (D2's rule keeps it so); no new runtime seam is expected. Plugin API 1.11.0 (additive) adds `TargetFacts.entities?: EntityFacts { projectileItems: readonly string[]; saddleTag: string }` (ADR 0071 D4 uses the second); both targets list their projectile item ids (26.4 adds `minecraft:ice_ball`, confirmed at implementation).
- **D8 Java eject (26.3 generator).** Generated entity classes extend the same vanilla base, set the same controls and navigation, copy `MsmRangedAttackGoal` as a template (like `FollowTypesGoal`), and implement `RangedAttackMob`.
- **D9 Not in this batch (limits).** Brain-driven mobs (allay item fetching, axolotl play dead, frog tongue, camel dash, piglin bartering); phantom swoops, ghast and vex flight and bat fluttering (each its own move control); crossbow charging poses and held weapons drawn in the mob's hand (needs an `ArmedModel` bone mapping, follow-up); mobs switching between melee and ranged by distance (both goals may be listed; priorities decide); fish buckets (`Bucketable` with a bucket item per mob and its client model); custom projectile entities with their own models (ADR 0056 follow-up).

## Consequences

- Format 1 grows additively: one top-level field, movement fields, one component, four goal types and two spawn rules. The corpus (prefix `entity-mx-`), the Codecs, the plugin, the generator and the app move together; a definition without the new fields registers and behaves as before.
- The runtime gains two driver classes, two GeckoLib subclasses and a `MobDriver` with one module per feature, so later features add a module rather than editing every class.
- Plugin API 1.11.0 is additive; built-in plugins keep `^1.0.0`.
- A project using any new field needs the runtime release that ships this.

## Findings

Recorded at close (2026-11-14) from B282–B291, B300 and the fixes after them (commits 18b02a5 to 6ab1558):

- **Flying speed.** A bee's `flying_speed` is 0.6; 0.4 is the parrot's. The default stays 0.4 (D3 corrected; the mcdoc and `EntityComponents.DEFAULT_FLYING_SPEED` say "a parrot's"). Fliers use `FlyingMoveControl(20, hovers)`, flying navigation with the bee's and parrot's path costs, no gravity, no fall damage (through `causeFallDamage`, never `checkFallDamage`) and `WaterAvoidingRandomFlyingGoal`.
- **Swim controls.** The fish's `SmoothSwimmingMoveControl` numbers are private, so every swimmer gets the dolphin's `SmoothSwimmingMoveControl(85, 10, 0.02, 0.1, true)`. Swimmers need `speed` near 1 (the dolphin's 1.2) to move well, so templates and flows use about 1. Swim and amphibious mobs get no float goal.
- **Drying out.** A swimmer without `breathes_air` dries out as a `WaterAnimal`: the first hurt comes at `dry_out_ticks` + 20 ticks out of water; out of water it flops. `breathes_air` adds `BreathAirGoal` and `TryFindLiquidGoal`. Amphibious mobs use `AmphibiousPathNavigation`.
- **Bodies.** `MsmEntity` (`PathfinderMob`), `MsmAnimal` (`TamableAnimal`) and `MsmMonster` (`Monster`), each `RangedAttackMob` and `PlayerRideableJumping` with its own `DATA_VARIANT` and `DATA_ANIM`, delegate to `MobDriver` and the modules in `registry/mob/` (`MobGoals`, `MobMovement`, `MobRanged`, `MobTaming`, `MobBreeding`, `MobRiding`, `MobVariants`, `MobBossBar`, `MobAnimations`); GeckoLib bodies `MsmGeo{Entity,Animal,Monster}` share `GeoControllers`. `EntityComponents` holds 16 fields, the `RecordCodecBuilder` limit: a seventeenth component needs a nested group. The six new components and four new goals are closed records; `msm:tameable` and `msm:breedable` on another base are one error each and dropped.
- **Registrar.** Attributes for every base add `tempt_range` 10 (only `Animal.createAnimalAttributes` had it), `flying_speed`, `step_height` only when given and `jump_strength` only for `msm:rideable.jump` above 0 (0 would stop the mob jumping at all). `createMob`'s default client tracking range is 5; boss mobs get 10. Spawn rules `animal` and `water_animal` repeat vanilla's bodies (identical on both versions); GameTest worlds have no fixed biome.
- **Ranged aim (M8).** Vanilla's bow and crossbow goals accept only monsters, so `MsmRangedAttackGoal(mob, speed, chargeTicks, intervalTicks, range, strafe)` is used on every base (interval counted shot to shot including the aim; shoots only in sight and range; strafes with `RangedBowAttackGoal`'s numbers). It aims at `getY(1/3)` plus the projectile's own drop `g·t·(t+1)/2`, because vanilla's 0.2 × distance overshoots at short range. Arrows go through `getMobArrow` and `setBaseDamage`; project throwables through `MsmThrownItem` with `setCarriesItem(false)`. An unknown projectile logs one runtime error per id at the first shot (a load-time check in the registrar is a follow-up). No crossbow charge pose and no weapon drawn in the hand.
- **Vehicle check (M1).** `ServerGamePacketListenerImpl.handleMoveVehicle` resets a vehicle whose packet moves it more than 10 blocks in a tick (squared 100, minus its delta movement) and logs "moved wrongly" above 0.0625 squared; the same on both versions. The format sets no upper bound on ridden `speed` (the planned cap of 5 was not added), so a very fast mount can be snapped back; a cap or a validator warning is a follow-up.
- **Ice ball.** The 26.4 projectile item is `minecraft:ice_ball` (`IceBallItem implements ProjectileItem`); plain `minecraft:potion` is not a projectile item.
- **Eject (D8).** The 26.3 generator copies the goal as `entity/MobRangedAttackGoal.java` (checked line by line against the runtime) and the drop formula, and covers bases, movement modes, spawn rules, the four goals and boss tracking range. It is stricter than the runtime: an unknown projectile, unknown keys and out-of-range values fail generation instead of being dropped. `VANILLA_PROJECTILE_ITEMS` is a local copy tied to the target facts by a test.

### Limits

The hard limits of the batch (M1–M17), referred to by the other mob ADRs and the guide:

| # | Limit | Why | What users see | Mitigation in this batch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | **Ridden mobs are moved by the rider's client.** | A player-controlled vehicle is client-authoritative in vanilla; the server receives its position (F9). | Steering, speed and jumping run the rider's copy of the definition; a server and a client with different content jar versions disagree silently. The server's vehicle movement check (more than 10 blocks in a tick) may snap very fast mounts back. | Same jar on both sides (Fabric registry sync already requires the ids); steering is an in-game check (B305); no cap on `speed` yet (follow-up). |
| M2 | **Server-side motion of a ridden mob is overwritten by the rider's client.** | Same class as the velocity problem (ADR 0052, audit 2.3). | `launch`, teleports or knockback applied to a ridden mount may be lost or jitter. | Recorded; in-game check of `launch` on a ridden mount; a vehicle sync like ADR 0052's is a follow-up if it fails. |
| M3 | **One triggered animation per tick in the vanilla renderer.** | `DATA_ANIM` is synched data: the client sees the last value of a tick. | Two `play_animation`s in one tick play only the second (GeckoLib plays both through its own packets). | Documented in the guide. |
| M4 | **Definitions are read separately on each side.** | Registry sync compares ids, not definition content. | A modpack that updates only the server jar shows wrong variant textures, renderer states or steering. | Guide note: update client and server together. |
| M5 | **No client test route.** | No OpenGL or Vulkan in the container (audit L16). | Drawing, animation feel and textures reach the user first. | Pure readers and the cross-language vertex golden (B285), animation conversion tests (B295), in-game checklist (B305). |
| M6 | **The vanilla renderer is a subset of GeckoLib.** | Vanilla keyframes have no Molang, easings or keyframe events (F12). | On 26.4 (and `renderer: vanilla`), Molang plays as 0, easings as linear; sound and particle keyframes are silent. | `entities.vanilla-renderer-subset` warning; presets stay inside the subset. |
| M7 | **Brain-driven and special-movement mobs are out of reach.** | Allays, axolotls, frogs, camels, sniffers, wardens, piglins use `Brain`; phantoms, ghasts, vexes, bats have their own move controls. | No item-fetching, play-dead, swooping or fluttering behaviour. | Goal-based equivalents only; listed in ADR 0070 D9. |
| M8 | **Vanilla bow and crossbow goals accept only monsters.** | Generic bounds (F4). | The runtime's own ranged goal is used on every base; no crossbow charge pose or weapon in hand. | `MsmRangedAttackGoal`; held items are a follow-up. |
| M9 | **Some vanilla methods changed between 26.3 and 26.4.** | F2, F6, F9 (`canBeABaby`, `setAgeLocked`, `travelFlying`, `checkFallDamage`, `jumpInLiquid`, `ItemSteerable`, `Equippable`). | None, as long as the runtime avoids them. | Never overridden or called; any later need goes behind a seam. |
| M10 | **The base class is fixed when the game starts.** | Registry freeze (audit L1) and single inheritance. | Changing `base` needs a restart; saved mobs of that type load into the new class and lose data the old class kept (an owner when leaving `animal`). | Validator info when an element's base changes is a follow-up; documented. |
| M11 | **Spawn caps are shared with vanilla mobs per category.** | `MobCategory` caps. | Many custom creatures thin out vanilla animals. | Documented. |
| M12 | **Boss bars reach tracking players only.** | `startSeenByPlayer` is per tracking player (the wither's rule). | Players beyond tracking range (10 chunks for bosses) see no bar; boss music plays only in the End (vanilla's rule). | `range` option; tracking range raised to 10. |
| M13 | **Saddles depend on a vanilla tag.** | The vanilla saddle equips only `#minecraft:can_equip_saddle` (F9). | A data pack that replaces that tag (`replace: true`) stops saddling custom mounts. | Documented; lowering appends with `merge: 'tag'`. |
| M14 | **GeckoLib does not scale babies or hide bones by itself.** | `GeoEntityRenderer` is not a `LivingEntityRenderer` (F15). | GeckoLib 5.5.7 never applies `getAgeScale`. | Fixed in the runtime's GeckoLib renderer (B296); in-game check. |
| M15 | **Ejected projects draw every mob with GeckoLib.** | The eject is 26.3-only (ADR 0072 D8). | A `vanilla` mob looks GeckoLib-drawn after eject (identical geometry, more animation features). | Info `export.eject-uses-geckolib`. |
| M16 | **No GeckoLib on 26.4.** | No build exists (F15). | `renderer: geckolib` mobs cannot be exported for 26.4. | `auto` falls back to the vanilla renderer. |
| M17 | **Modpacks with GeckoLib on one side only.** | Separate-runtime jars let a pack ship GeckoLib to the client only or the server only. | `auto` mobs use different classes on each side; synched data agrees (GeckoLib's `GeoEntity` defines none), but GeckoLib's animation packets may be refused by a client without GeckoLib. | Guide note: ship GeckoLib to both sides or neither. |

## Status

Accepted (2026-11-14). Defaults the user may veto: the field name `base` and its values (D1); absent `base` meaning `mob` rather than derived from the components (D1); the movement defaults (D3); the ranged defaults copying a skeleton (D4); the component name `msm:ranged_attack` (D4).
