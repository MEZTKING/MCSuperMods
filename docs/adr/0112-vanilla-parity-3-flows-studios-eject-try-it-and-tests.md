# ADR 0112: Vanilla parity 3: flows, Studios, the eject, Try it and tests

## Context

ADRs [0103](0103-growing-plants-and-project-flower-pots.md) to [0111](0111-commands-rules-game-rules-and-stats-2.md) add four plant types and project flower pots, six weathering types, lightning, three storage options, own block sounds and set types, tool and burning pairs, the last data and resource pack kinds, four data-only flows, trader reputation, demand and work sites, villager homes and types, fluid mixing, cauldrons and bottles, fish buckets, two shoulders, two flight modes and five habits, boats, command arguments in Rules, more argument types, six triggers, dialogs, game rules, statistics and tab order. As in [ADR 0101](0101-vanilla-parity-2-flows-studios-try-it-and-tests.md), each needs Studios, Creator flows, Very easy wording, templates, examples, Try it lines, a fixture with parity tests and e2e paths. The Java eject still leaves out vanilla parity 2 batch 2 (limit Q28; `NOT_IN_EJECT_KINDS` and `NOT_IN_EJECT_COMPONENTS` in `packages/exporters/src/java-project/index.ts`, fact H16). The working plan was `docs/PLAN-vanilla-parity-3.md` (briefs B503–B546), deleted at the close of batch 2 (B544); its fact table and limits are the last sections of this ADR.

## Decision

- **D1 Studios.** Batch 1: the Block Studio's type picker and sections for the five new types, the pot's plant list, weathering on the new types, the chest's **Double chest**, **Different for every player** and **Holds water**, a **Sounds** section and own set type sounds, a **Vanilla block rules** addition Studio. Batch 2: the Trader section's reputation, demand and work site; the Profession Studio's Very easy list; a **Villager look** Studio (`msm:villager_type`); the Mob Studio's **Bucket**, **Habits**, both shoulders and the two flight modes; the Fluid Studio's mixing, cauldron and bottle and its Very easy list; the Item Studio's boat section; the Command Studio's new argument types; **Game rule** and **Statistic** Studios; the Creative tab Studio's hand order. Labels in plain British English (the plan's "Very easy wording").
- **D2 Flows.** New Creator flows "Cave plants", "Desert plants", "Flower pot", "Banner pattern", "Armour trim", "Glider", "Pot pattern", "Villager look" and "Game rule"; new answers in the Copper set, Storage crate, Wood set (own door sounds, boats), Wandering merchant, Village job, Water mob, Pet, Flying mob, Mount, Fluid, Custom command and Creative tab flows. Every picture drawn by the art generator ([ADR 0092](0092-minecraft-style-art.md)). Templates and examples for each new kind and type; the `examples/` mirror regenerated once per batch.
- **D3 The eject.** The 26.3 Java generator covers everything of vanilla parity 2 batch 2 (professions and job site POIs, traders, mount storage, shoulder pets, fluids with buckets) and of this plan (the plant types, pots, weathering types, lightning scraping by a Fabric event, the storage options, own sounds and set types, pairs, villager types and homes, buckets, flight modes and habits, fluid mixing, cauldrons and bottles, boats, game rules, statistics, argument types, tab order). What needs a Mixin or a script stays out with `export.not-in-eject` (the Mixin events of ADR 0111 D3, a Mixin route for lightning). Each generated shared class is compared line by line with the runtime; the ejected fixtures pass the same parity tests as their content jars. 26.4 still has no `javaProject`.
- **D4 Tests.** Fixtures `fixtures/sample-plants-and-storage` (batch 1) and `fixtures/sample-mobs-and-commands` (batch 2) with parity tests on both content jars and the 26.3 ejected jars, plus `fixtures/sample-villagers-and-fluids` on its ejected jar; GameTests per feature on both versions; client GameTest screenshots of the new looks; CI exports, server-checks and parity-runs both fixtures on both versions; e2e `plants-path`, `storage-2-path`, `villagers-2-path`, `mobs-2-path`, `commands-2-path`, and `eject-path` extended.
- **D5 Try it.** The new plants, pots, double chests, buckets, both shoulders, hovering mobs, boats and cauldrons are drawn and usable in the preview as far as the sim allows; reputation shows in the merchant mock-up's prices; mixing, lightning and the six new events are log lines in "What happened" (limit N24); sim-parity scenarios for the new behaviours.
- **D6 Spikes first.** B503 (batch 1) and B524 and B525 (batch 2) answer X1–X34 before anything depending on them starts; their answers can change the ADRs (recorded as amendments, as ADR 0098 and 0099 were).
- **D7 Fact table and limits.** The plan's facts H1–H16 and limits N1–N26 move into this ADR at the close of batch 2 (B544), with an outcome column.

## Consequences

- The eject catches up with the content jar for everything server-side; the escape hatch is no longer missing batch 2.
- Two more fixtures in CI on both versions (longer CI runs).
- The asar and renderer budgets are re-checked in each app and library brief; the gzip reserve (ADR 0079 D4) is the next step if they are reached.

## What the plan dropped, and why

Moved from the plan's gap table at B544, so nobody plans these again without a new reason.

- **Not in vanilla** (the golden rule does not ask for them): gravity and sky textures per dimension as a setting (Q27: vanilla has neither in data; the skybox choice is in the Dimension Type Studio and gravity works by vanilla's own way, a function with `/attribute ... minecraft:gravity`, which the guide shows); pipes and tanks, buoyancies other than water's and lava's, waterlogging by a project fluid; hopper access to mount storage and bags; containers on slabs, doors and fences; candle cakes on project cakes; cancelling vanilla commands and argument types beyond vanilla's; new trunk and foliage placer shapes.
- **Closed already or no pack content:** the trial spawner kind (a generic kind); `slot_source` (a schema with no data folder or registry on either target); `gpu_warnlist` and `regional_compliancies` (game-internal files).
- **Client-only rendering, for the next client batch:** fog colours on 26.3; custom screens and block entity renderers; more than three machine slots; the portal overlay; particle trails and per-spawn options; held weapons and the bow pose; variants that change the model; client commands; scripts in the eject (B142).
- **Left out as brain code** (limit N15): the warden's sonic boom and sniffing, the frog's tongue, the piglin's whole brain, sculk vibrations of our own.
