# ADR 0109: Mobs 2: fish buckets, both shoulders, hovering and phasing flight, and five habits

## Context

The mob expansion ([ADR 0070](0070-mob-bodies-movement-and-ranged-attacks.md) D9, [ADR 0071](0071-tameable-breedable-rideable-mobs-variants-and-boss-bars.md) D10) and [ADR 0098](0098-traders-professions-mount-storage-and-shoulder-pets.md) left out vanilla mob abilities that are server-side: fish caught in a bucket (cod, salmon, axolotl, tadpole), a pet on each shoulder (vanilla parrots use both), special flight (the ghast and blaze hovering, the vex flying through walls while charging, the phantom swooping, the bat roosting), and brain-driven behaviours. At f6a8adc mobs have four movement modes and ten goal types, no bucket component and one shoulder (fact H14). Held weapons, the bow pose and variants that change the model are client rendering and stay for the client batch.

Brain behaviours are many; five are visible, common in mods and reachable with goals over vanilla's own entity calls: the allay collecting items for its owner, the sniffer digging up items, the axolotl playing dead, the camel's ridden dash and the piglin's bartering. The warden's sonic boom and sniffing, the frog's tongue and the piglin's whole brain (hunting, admiring, alliances) are not (limit N15).

## Decision

- **D1 Fish buckets.** Component `msm:bucketable { item_name?, water?: boolean }` on `swim` mobs (limit N12): a water bucket used on the mob takes it into a bucket item `<path>_bucket` (generated id), which keeps its custom name, health and variant and releases it with the water, as vanilla's bucketable contract does (X20).
- **D2 Both shoulders.** `msm:shoulder_riding` gains `sides?: "left" | "right" | "either"` (default `either`); a player carries at most two pets, one per side, beside vanilla's parrots on the sides they leave free (X21; limit N13).
- **D3 Flight modes.** `MovementMode` gains `hover` (floats at `hover_height` above the ground, default 4 blocks; ghast-like and blaze-like) and `phase` (flies, and passes through blocks while its attack goal charges; vex-like). Goal types gain `swoop` (circles high, then dives at its target; phantom-like) and `roost` (hangs under a block in daylight or when idle; bat-like), `roost` only if the runtime's renderer can draw the pose (X22; else the client batch, limit N14).
- **D4 Five habits** as goal types: `collect_items` (picks up items matching the one it was given and brings them to its owner or a note block it heard; allay-like), `dig_items` (digs on its `dig_blocks`, drops a roll of `loot_table`; sniffer-like), `play_dead` (lies still and regenerates when hurt below a share of its health; axolotl-like), `barter` (takes `item` from a player or the ground and drops a roll of `loot_table`; piglin-like), and on `msm:rideable` the field `dash` (a charged forward leap from vanilla's ridden jump bar; camel-like). Each is vanilla behaviour over our numbers, learned by observation (X23).
- **D5 Format and sync.** New synced flags only where the renderer needs them (playing dead, roosting, dashing), as section 2.3 of the coverage audit asks.

## Consequences

- The most-asked vanilla mob abilities without brains are possible; Water mob, Pet, Flying mob and Mount flows gain answers.
- More goals to keep on both versions; each has a GameTest and a parity case.
- The brain-only behaviours stay a stated limit.

## Amendment after the spike (B524, 2026-11-22)

Measured on both versions (evidence in `docs/reference/spikes/b524/`).

- **D1 buckets: go.** `Bucketable` on the swim body with a `MobBucketItem` (`stacksTo(1)`, `BUCKET_ENTITY_DATA`); name, health, variant and persistence come back on release.
- **D2 both shoulders: go, with a correction.** The runtime's shoulder pet today sits on the player's right (seen from behind, the side of vanilla's right parrot), though its documentation says left. Sides are named by the player's view, and a second pet takes the mirrored side; each tick a pet takes the side vanilla's parrots leave free (parrots take left first).
- **D3 flight: go.** Hover by scanning down for a collision shape (not the heightmap) with a damped controller; phasing by `noPhysics` around the tick while charging; roosting under a block with no gravity. **Limit N14 is lifted:** the roosting pose is vanilla's upside-down flip, drawn by the runtime's own renderer from one synced flag, no client batch needed.
- **D4 habits: go for all five.** Collect (thrown items carry a thrower and are skipped), dig (`GIFT` loot context), play dead (one synced flag for the pose), barter (`PIGLIN_BARTER` with the mob only, never an origin), dash (vanilla's jump bar, applied in `tickRidden`, about 7 blocks at a full bar).

## Findings at close (B544, 2026-11-22)

Recorded at the close of vanilla parity 3 batch 2 (B544) from the code on the session branch at a57dbd3 and the plan's notes: B524 (a11bfed, the spike; amendment c473792), B526 (a8a416b), B527 (04d9019), B530 (1098db0), B532 (91c4d2b), B537b (2bf7321), B538 (054e871), B539 (851ff8e), B540 (074e78e), eject 2c (d51e5c7), B541 (f565cf9, a57dbd3) and B543 (66043fb). In the 1.5.3 test build except eject 2c; awaiting the user's in-game checks (`fixtures/sample-mobs-and-commands/README.md`).

- **Spike answers** (B524, both versions; `docs/reference/spikes/b524/`). **X20:** the runtime's swim body with vanilla's `Bucketable` and a `MobBucketItem` is caught with a water bucket and released with its name, health and flag. **X21:** vanilla parrots take the left slot first, then the right; the runtime's pet sat on the player's right shoulder (seen from behind) though `MobShoulder` called it the left; a second pet on the mirrored side works, and the runtime must pick the side the parrots leave free each tick. **X22:** hover and phase work on our bodies (no collision around the tick while charging, no suffocation damage); the runtime's own renderer already draws vanilla's upside-down flip, so roosting needs one synced flag and no client batch. **X23:** all five habits work on our bodies: collecting (an item thrown to the owner must carry its thrower, or it is picked up again), digging (`gameplay/sniffer_digging` with the `GIFT` context), playing dead (untargetable, regenerating), bartering (`gameplay/piglin_bartering`; `PIGLIN_BARTER` takes no origin) and the ridden dash through vanilla's jump bar (6.9 blocks forward at scale 0.9); only roosting and playing dead want a render flag.
- **D1 as built** (B527, B530, B532). `msm:bucketable { item_name?, water? }`: every mob body implements vanilla's `Bucketable` and hands it to `registry/mob/MobBuckets`; the item `<ns>:<path>_bucket` is vanilla's `MobBucketItem` (water, stacks to 1), or with `water: false` the runtime's `DryBucketItem` (an empty bucket takes the mob and puts it back on a block's face, with no water). The bucket keeps the custom name, health, variant and "from a bucket" (it never despawns). The item sits in the Tools and Utilities tab after vanilla's buckets and is named "Bucket of <name>". The component on a mob that does not swim is dropped with a warning by the runtime and is a validator error in the app (limit N12).
- **D2 as built.** `msm:shoulder_riding.sides` (`left`, `right`, `either`; default `either`), named by the player's own view; `MobShoulder.Side` and the synced `DATA_PERCH_LEFT`; a player carries two pets at most, `either` takes the right first, and vanilla's parrots keep their shoulders (a pet takes the side they leave free each tick). The earlier documentation that called the right shoulder "left" is corrected.
- **D3 as built.** Movement modes `hover` (`MobMovement.HoverMoveControl`: scans down for a collision shape, a liquid's surface counting as ground, and closes a share of the height error each tick, because the spike's controller bobbed; `hover_height` default 4) and `phase` (flies, and `goals/PhaseChargeGoal` switches collision off around the tick while charging, until the mob is out of the block it charged into). Goals `swoop` (`SwoopGoal`: circles about 6 blocks up for 60 to 120 ticks, then dives; only on `fly`, `hover` or `phase`) and `roost` (`RoostGoal`: hangs under a block by day or when idle, lets go when hurt or when a player comes within 4 blocks, with a 100-tick cooldown). **Limit N14 is lifted**, as the amendment says: the pose is drawn by `MsmVanillaRenderer` from the synced `DATA_ROOSTING`.
- **D4 as built.** Goals `collect_items` (`CollectItemsGoal`: the given item is kept in a saved field, not in the hand; deliveries go to the owner, or to a note block it heard for anyone), `dig_items` (`DigForItemsGoal`: 40 ticks of digging, then `interval_ticks`), `play_dead` (`PlayDeadGoal`, priority 0 so it takes over any running goal; one synced flag `DATA_PLAYING_DEAD` for the pose) and `barter` (`BarterGoal`), all over `registry/mob/MobHabits`; `msm:rideable.dash` in `MobRiding` (1.6 x the bar's power forward and 0.3 up, applied in `tickRidden`; it replaces the jump when both are set and plays no sound, because vanilla's camel dash sound is not a plain sound event). D4 counts five habits; with `swoop` and `roost` the product speaks of six habit goals and the dash. Save keys: `FromBucket`, `PerchSide`, `Roosting` (written only while the mob hangs), `PlayDeadTicks`, `CollectLiked`, `CollectCarried`, `CollectOwner`, `DigCooldown`, `BarterHeld`, `BarterTicks`.
- **D5 as built.** Three synced values were added: the perch side, roosting and playing dead. The dash needs none (vanilla's jump bar carries it).
- **Validators** (B530). `msm.entities.bucketable`, `.flight` and `.habits` (8 codes, `ENTITIES_MOB2_PROBLEM_CODES`): bucketable only on `swim`, `dash` only on walk or amphibious mounts, `swoop` only on a flying mode, a barter or dig goal naming a loot table that exists.
- **The rest of the product** (ADR 0112 findings): the Mob Studio's **Bucket** and **Habits** sections, hover and phase in Movement and the Shoulder picker; ten templates in `templates/mobs-2`; answers in the Pet (either shoulder, collect, dig), Water mob (bucket, play dead), Flying mob (hover, phase, swoop, roost), Mount (dash) and Wandering merchant (barter) flows; the 26.3 eject of buckets, hover, phase, the dash and the six habit goals (B537b) and of shoulder pets (eject 2c); Try it's fish buckets, shoulder pets, hover and phase.
- **Tests.** `MsmMob2GameTest` on both versions (13 tests); the client GameTest `MsmMobs2ClientGameTest` (hovering, roosting upside down, playing dead on its side, two pets on both shoulders from the front: screenshot `msm_shoulders_<mc>`); eleven parity cases, one per mob of the fixture, on the content jars of both versions and the 26.3 ejected jar.
- **Traps found.** A goal that searches with `tickCount % n` may never fire, because the goal selector asks a waiting goal only every other tick: the habits count `canUse` calls instead. Mock players are never on the ground; tests set it. An idle roost starts by a 1 in 40 chance at each goal check, so `theCaveFlitterHangsUnderABlockAndWakesWhenHurt` was given 600 ticks and a full ceiling after failing now and then (a57dbd3; still on the watch list).
- **Limits.** N12 (buckets for `swim` mobs only), N13 (two shoulder pets at most, tameable `animal` mobs) and N15 (no warden, frog tongue, piglin brain or sculk behaviours) hold. An ejected mob is drawn by GeckoLib and keeps its normal pose while it roosts or plays dead (the guide says so). GeckoLib-drawn mobs in the content jar were not checked for the two poses.
- **Open follow-ups.** Swoop, roost, collect, dig, barter, play dead and the dash in Try it (said as "only in the game" today); the two poses on GeckoLib renderers; a dash sound; the cave flitter test, and if it still misses, `RoostGoal`'s idle chance or a test that starts the roost itself; `RestockAtWorkSiteGoal` could move into `MobGoals`.

## Status

Accepted with findings (2026-11-22, close B544; in the 1.5.3 test build, awaiting the user's in-game checks). The user has not yet been shown the defaults and may still veto them. Vetoable defaults as built: buckets for `swim` mobs only, in the Tools and Utilities tab, with a dry bucket for `water: false`; two shoulder pets at most, `either` taking the right shoulder first and parrots keeping theirs; the hover height of 4 and a liquid's surface counting as ground; a phasing mob passing through blocks until it is out of the one it charged into; the roost's wake rules and 100-tick cooldown; `play_dead` at priority 0; a collector delivering only for its owner (to a note block for anyone); a dash that replaces the jump and plays no sound; these six habit goals and the dash, and no brain behaviours. Proposed at first (2026-11-22) with these defaults: five habits (and which five); the hover height of 4; buckets for `swim` mobs only; two shoulder pets at most; `roost` dropped to the client batch if the pose cannot be drawn.
