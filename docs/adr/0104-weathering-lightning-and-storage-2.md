# ADR 0104: Weathering on more types, lightning, and storage 2

## Context

[ADR 0094](0094-remaining-vanilla-block-types-waterlogging-and-weathering.md) D8 gave copper-like ageing to eleven types (`BlockType.WEATHERING_TYPES`: block, pillar, horizontal, directional, slab, stairs, door, trapdoor, pane, chain, lantern) and left out the rest ([ADR 0101](0101-vanilla-parity-2-flows-studios-try-it-and-tests.md) limit Q7). Vanilla's copper family also has a lightning rod (a rod), bars (a pane: covered), a bulb (a block: covered), a chest (a container) and a golem statue; vanilla's lightning scrapes copper, which MSM's project copper ignores because vanilla's maps hold vanilla blocks only (limit Q6; fact H2). Containers and machines cannot hold water (limit Q8, fact H3), though vanilla's chests can. [ADR 0074](0074-containers-and-block-entities.md) D10 left out double chests and per-player storage like an ender chest, both vanilla.

## Decision

- **D1 Six more weathering types.** `rod`, `button`, `pressure_plate`, `fence`, `fence_gate` and `wall` join `WEATHERING_TYPES`, each a small subclass of its type's class that implements vanilla's change-over-time behaviour, as ADR 0094 D8's eleven do. Vanilla has no copper buttons, plates, fences, gates or walls; they are added because the mechanism is the same subclass and a set with holes would surprise users (vetoable). Waxing and scraping as ADR 0094 D9.
- **D2 Lightning scrapes project copper.** A lightning strike on or next to a project weathering block takes it (and its neighbours) back by the same rule vanilla applies to copper: as observed beside vanilla copper in the spike (B503, X5), not transcribed. The route is a Fabric event if one exists on both versions, else one head injection under DESIGN section 10's Mixin policy, with a justification comment and a GameTest. Copper golems keep vanilla's maps (limit N5).
- **D3 Containers and machines hold water.** `msm:waterloggable` is allowed with `msm:container` and `msm:machine` on the four `MsmBlock` types; the block keeps its block entity, menu and hopper faces (X7).
- **D4 Double chests.** The `chest` layout gains `double: boolean` (default false; only with `rows: 3`, as vanilla's two chests make six rows; limit N6). Two such blocks placed side by side join: a `type` state (`single`, `left`, `right`), block models for each (no block entity renderer, ADR 0074 C9 stays), one six-row menu over both halves, hoppers and comparators per half as vanilla's. Sneak-placing keeps them apart, as vanilla.
- **D5 Per-player storage.** The `chest` layout gains `per_player: boolean` (default false): each player sees their own contents, kept in a Fabric data attachment on the player per block id (as vanilla's ender chest has one store for all ender chests; limit N7), kept across death and dimension change. Not with `keep_contents` or `double`; hoppers do not reach it (as vanilla's ender chest).
- **D6 Weathering storage.** `msm:weathering` is allowed on containers (a copper-chest-like block); ageing replaces the block and keeps the block entity's contents (X13).
- **D7 Format.** Fields in the existing component groups; new block component keys go into the nested group of ADR 0074 C18.

## Consequences

- The copper family is complete apart from the golem statue (a mob-shaped block: client rendering, later).
- Containers gain three options that change their saved shape (`type` state, the player store): removing them from a used block follows ADR 0074 C7's warning.
- One Mixin more is possible (D2), with its GameTest.

## Amendment after the spike (B503, 2026-11-22)

Measured on both versions (evidence in `docs/reference/spikes/b503/`).

- **Lightning: go, no Mixin.** Fabric's `ServerEntityEvents.ENTITY_LOAD` fires for the bolt on its spawn tick before the strike; the runtime scrapes project weathering blocks from there. Vanilla's measured rule: the struck block goes to its first stage, and a few neighbours (4 to 19 cells on a 7 by 7 floor) go back one stage; with a rod, the block under it drops 0 to 3 stages (full reach: verify at implementation). Visual-only bolts cannot be told apart (no getter) and are treated alike.
- **Weathering on more types: go.** Rod, button, plate, fence, gate and wall subclasses age at vanilla copper's rate and keep every state property.
- **Waterlogged containers: go.** `MsmContainerBlock` with `SimpleWaterloggedBlock` keeps its block entity, menu, hoppers and comparator; breaking it leaves a water source.
- **Double chests: go**, as a `ChestBlock` subclass family with its own `ChestBlockEntity` type (overriding `newBlockEntity`), not `MsmContainerBlock`; one 54-slot menu, each half drops its own items.
- **Per-player storage: go**, as a persistent Fabric attachment copied on death, one entry per block id.
- **Ageing containers.** `Weathering.replace` and the random tick drop the contents; the runtime copies the whole block entity data, sets the new block with `UPDATE_SKIP_BLOCK_ENTITY_SIDEEFFECTS` and pastes it back.

## Findings at close (B523, 2026-11-22)

Recorded at the close of vanilla parity 3 batch 1 (B523) from the code on the session branch at 4ede504 and the plan's notes: B503 (0af2603, the spike), B506 (92ed96f), B510 (bd41469), B512 (c529ef4), B513 (4536398), B515 (bc6f27e), B516 (6f544a0), B517 (9646bad), B518 (a8d50f5), B519 (3b94abc), B520 (c01b166), B521 (3e92e0a) and the merge findings (3cfb60e). Unreleased; awaiting the user's in-game checks (`fixtures/sample-plants-and-storage/README.md`).

- **Spike answers** (B503, both versions; `docs/reference/spikes/b503/`). **X5:** a lightning strike does not scrape a project weathering block by itself (vanilla's maps); Fabric's `ServerEntityEvents.ENTITY_LOAD` sees the bolt on its spawn tick before the strike, so no Mixin is needed. **X6:** rod, button, pressure plate, fence, fence gate and wall subclasses tick randomly and age at copper's rate, keeping every state property. **X7:** a waterlogged runtime container keeps its block entity, menu, hopper faces and comparator, and leaves a water source when broken. **X8:** a double chest works as a `ChestBlock` subclass family with its own block entity type (overriding `newBlockEntity`) and block models, one 54-slot menu, each half dropping its own items. **X9:** a persistent Fabric attachment on the player, one entry per block id, copied on death, keeps a store per player across death and dimension change. **X13:** `Weathering.replace` and the random tick drop a container's contents; copying the block entity data and setting the new block with `UPDATE_SKIP_BLOCK_ENTITY_SIDEEFFECTS` keeps them. **X14:** a vanilla spawner set to a project mob spawns it, and a project spawn egg used on a spawner sets it (survival and creative): nothing to build, a guide line only.
- **D1 as built** (B506, B510, B512). `BlockType.WEATHERING_TYPES` has 17 types (rod, button, pressure plate, fence, fence gate and wall added), each with a `registry/weathering/MsmWeathering*Block` subclass; copper's rate (0.0436 per forced tick in the GameTests); `Weathering.afterChange` reschedules a pressed button's or plate's release. Lowering and the Studio's "Ages like copper" cover the six types; the Copper set flow gains a rod, button, pressure plate, fence, fence gate, wall and a joinable chest (defaults unchanged, `COPPER_SET_DEFAULT_MEMBERS`).
- **D2 as built** (B510). `registry/weathering/Lightning.strike(ServerLevel, BlockPos, RandomSource)` from `ENTITY_LOAD`: the struck block goes to its first stage, then 3 to 5 walks of 1 to 8 steps take neighbours one stage back (about 9 to 12 neighbours a bolt on a 7 by 7 floor, as vanilla copper measured 4 to 19). Waxed blocks are ignored; a walk passes only through project blocks; visual-only bolts scrape too (they cannot be told apart); project rods do not attract lightning (only vanilla's lightning rod does); copper golems keep vanilla's maps (limit N5). The rod's full reach under vanilla's rule was not measured (verify at implementation). No Mixin was added.
- **D3 as built** (B513, B515, B521). `MsmWaterloggedContainerBlock` (from `Waterlogging.blockFor`) for `msm:waterloggable` with `msm:container` or `msm:machine` on the four shaped types; `BlockType.WATERLOGGABLE_TYPES` is unchanged. A double chest always holds water, as vanilla's chests do: the Studio says so (`block-waterlogged-double-chest`) instead of showing a switch that did nothing.
- **D4 as built** (B513, B515). `MsmDoubleChestBlock` and `MsmDoubleChestBlockEntity` (own type, `BlockEntityHooks.registerDoubleChest`); states `facing`, `type` (`single`, `left`, `right`) and `waterlogged`; `on_open` and `on_close` fire from the half whose `type` is not `right`; a broken half drops its own 27; a double chest ignores `msm:shape` and `msm:redstone` (warned). Lowering writes closed variants for each `type` (`withDoubleChestVariants`); validator `msm.blocks-items.storage-2` (5 codes) refuses `double` outside a plain or horizontal block with 3 rows (limit N6). Left and right were checked in the game against Try it (B520).
- **D5 as built** (B513). `PlayerStores` (attachment `msm_runtime:player_stores`, copied on death; in the eject `<mod id>:player_stores`), keyed by the family's first stage, so the stages of an ageing private chest share a store; no hoppers and no comparator output, as the ender chest. Not with `keep_contents` or `double`.
- **D6 as built** (B513). `MsmWeatheringContainerBlock` (and `.Waterlogged`) and `MsmDoubleChestBlock.Weathers`; `Weathering.replace` copies the whole block entity data (contents, custom name, lock), a double chest's other half first, so ageing, waxing, scraping and lightning keep contents and both halves change together. A warning when weathering stages differ in rows or size (items beyond the smaller size are lost). **Machines cannot age:** the runtime drops `msm:weathering` and `msm:waxable` on a machine with a log line, and since the merge findings the app warns `blocks-items.machine-cannot-age`.
- **D7** holds: the fields sit in the chest layout (`double`, `per_player`) and the existing component groups.
- **Tests** (B510, B513, B520). `MsmWeatheringGameTest` asserts containers hold water and age; the parity cases bronze ageing with state kept, wax, axe and lightning, the twin crate's menu and halves, the bronze chest ageing with its contents, the private crate for two players and the sea crate's water; `MsmPlants3ClientGameTest` opens a six-row chest screen from the use key and shows a waterlogged crate. Flaky-test watch: `aBoltScrapesProjectBlocksAsItScrapesVanillaCopper` compares two random means over 12 bolts.
- **Open follow-ups.** A level-event check (3003, 3004, 3005) for waxing and scraping in parity; e2e for storage on 26.4; the copper golem statue (client rendering, later); hopper reach on a per-player chest stays off as in vanilla.

## Status

Accepted with findings (2026-11-22, close B523; unreleased, awaiting the user's in-game checks). The user has not yet been shown the defaults and may still veto them. Vetoable defaults as built: weathering on buttons, plates, fences, gates and walls although vanilla has none; lightning scraping by vanilla's observed rule, visual-only bolts included, waxed blocks and project rods left alone; doubles only for three-row chests, always holding water; one player store per block family, copied on death, out of hoppers' reach; ageing containers keeping their contents; machines not ageing (a warning). Proposed at first (2026-11-22) with these defaults: weathering on buttons, plates, fences, gates and walls although vanilla has none; lightning scraping by vanilla's observed rule; doubles only for three-row chests; one player store per block id.
