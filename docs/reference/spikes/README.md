# Spike evidence

Results of the throwaway spikes of vanilla parity 2, batch 2, and vanilla parity 3, kept so the briefs that build on them (B472–B485 of vanilla parity 2, batch 2; the plan was deleted at B485, its facts and limits are in ADR 0101) can read what was measured and reuse code that worked. The spike code was deleted from `engine-mod/`; these are copies. Nothing here is built, linted or shipped. Only MSM's own code and our own measurements are kept: no vanilla files, decompiled code, `javap` listings or screenshots of the game.

The decisions taken from them are in the ADR amendments, which win over anything here:

- `b469/`: traders, professions, mount storage and shoulder pets (S11–S13), measured on 26.3 and 26.4-snapshot-7. Decisions in `docs/adr/0098-traders-professions-mount-storage-and-shoulder-pets.md`, "Amendment after the spike". `spike-src/` holds the spike's Java (`spike3/`, the `SpikePoi` seam for each version) and resources; `server-*.json` are the GameTest observations by key (`S11.*`, `S12.*`, `S13.*`).
- `b470/`: custom fluids (S14), measured on both versions, with and without the 26.4 `Entity.isInWater`/`isInLava` Mixin. Decisions in `docs/adr/0099-custom-fluids.md`, "Amendment after the spike". `spike-src/` holds `spike4/` (fluids, liquid block, Mixin, client), the 26.3 `SpikeFluid` seam and resources; `server-*.json` and `client-*.json` are the observations (`client-*`: `sp.*` singleplayer, `ds.*` a dedicated server with a joined client).
- `b503/`: vanilla parity 3, batch 1 (X1–X14 of the vanilla parity 3 plan, deleted at B544; the answers are in the findings of ADRs 0103–0105: growing and column plants, dripleaf, lichen, project flower pots, lightning on project copper, weathering on six more types, a waterlogged container, a double chest, per-player storage, own block sounds and set types, strip, flatten, till, wax, age and burn pairs for vanilla blocks, an ageing container, spawners), measured on 26.3 and 26.4-snapshot-7. Decisions go into ADRs 0103–0105 from the B503 report. `spike-src/` holds `spike3/` (the spike mod `B503Mod` with namespace `msm_b503`, one GameTest class per question, the client GameTest), the `B503Version` seam for each version, the temporary `fabric.mod.json`, the `supports_*` tag additions, the `block_sound_set` entry and `sounds.json`; `server-*.json` are the GameTest observations by key (`X1.*` … `X14.*`), `client-*.json` the client GameTest's (screenshot names, client light, the sounds the client played). The client GameTest needs `ALSOFT_DRIVERS=null` besides the EGL and Xvfb settings, or OpenAL finds no device and no sound reaches a `SoundEventListener`.
- `b524/`: vanilla parity 3, batch 2, villagers, mobs and boats (X15–X24 of the vanilla parity 3 plan, deleted at B544; the answers are in the findings of ADRs 0107, 0109 and 0110: reputation and special prices, demand, restocking at a work site, project beds as homes, villager types, fish buckets, both shoulders, hovering, phasing and roosting, five habits, boats and chest boats), measured on 26.3 and 26.4-snapshot-7. Decisions are in ADRs 0107, 0109 and 0110, "Amendment after the spike (B524)" and "Findings at close (B544)". `spike-src/` holds `spike4/` (the spike mod `B524Mod` with namespace `msm_b524`: `SpikeMob`, one runtime `MsmEntity` body per behaviour, `SpikeFish` with vanilla's bucketable contract, the boat types and items, the villager type; `B524Client` registers vanilla's boat renderer through the runtime's `EntityRendererRegistration` seam; three GameTest classes and one client GameTest), the temporary `fabric.mod.json` and `resources/make-resources.py`, which writes the spike's own flat pictures (boat, chest boat, villager and zombie villager type layers) and its `b524_yard` structure; `server-*.json` are the GameTest observations by key (`X15.*` … `X24`), `client-*.json` the client GameTest's (`client.x15` … `client.x24`, screenshot names). No version seam was needed.

- `b525/`: vanilla parity 3, batch 2 (X25–X34 of the vanilla parity 3 plan, deleted at B544; the answers are in the findings of ADRs 0108 and 0111: fluid mixing by the liquid block's own check, project cauldrons and vanilla's empty cauldron, bottles filled and drunk, vanilla boats on project fluids, resource packs over a project fluid's pictures, 32 command argument types on the server and in the client's tree, own game rules, custom statistics, the crafting, pickup, eating, sleeping and advancement hooks, data pack dialogs), measured on 26.3 and 26.4-snapshot-7. Decisions go into ADRs 0108 and 0111 from the B525 report. `spike-src/` holds `spike5/` (the spike mod `B525Mod` with namespace `msm_b525`, one GameTest class per question, the client GameTest, the liquid block and cauldrons) and `spike5/mixin/` (the throwaway Mixins, each with its justification), the temporary `fabric.mod.json` and Mixin config, the spike's advancements, functions, dialog and lang, and the fluid tag files as they were during the spike; `server-*.json` are the GameTest observations by key (`X25.*` … `X34.*`), `client-*.json` the client GameTest's (command trees before and after `/reload`, the dialog screen, pixel counts of the resource pack screenshots, game rules and a statistic after reopening the world, a night's sleep). `client-26.3-structure-resource-argument.txt` records the one failed run (a `ResourceArgument` over the structure registry disconnects every client).

- `b565/`: the Showcase plan's measurements (Z1–Z13 of ADR 0118, whose plan is deleted: example lowering, mount to first frame, frame rates, mount churn and memory, stills from the canvas, reach, driving, portals, Rules in the script worker, crops, the machine, a fluid, silence), measured on mc-26.3 and mc-26.4 under SwiftShader. Decisions go into ADR 0118 from the B565 report. `clips.json` holds the action sequence and camera keys per clip; `results/` the numbers; `stills/` the ten mc-26.3 stills (MSM's own drawing, no client jar); `spike-src/` the throwaway clip player, harness and specs (not built).

- `b574/`: the live world generation spike (WS1–WS18 of the live world generation plan, since closed into ADR 0119: deepslate 0.27.2 against real worlds of the vanilla server on 26.3 and 26.4-snapshot-7, speed in a worker, what it lacks, the jar paths; a go with ten fixes). Its README has the answers; the decisions and what the built code reached are in ADR 0119.

- `b547/`: the game modes spike (Y1–Y16 of the game modes plan, now in ADR 0117: void worlds, `dimension_origin` start structures, first join, the void Nether and End, one block, teams, areas and maps on vanilla servers and a client GameTest, both versions). It found that MSM's exported structure templates placed as air on 26.x.
- `b600/`: the Live views 2 (data) spike (2S1–2S15 of `docs/PLAN-live-views.md`: loot odds against 10,000 game rolls, tags, predicates, advancement layout, dialogs, death lines, enchantment costs, on both versions; a go for ADR 0120).
- `b619/`: the Live views 3 (looks and sounds) spike (3S1–3S12 of `docs/PLAN-live-views.md`: the asset store and every vanilla sound decoded, stand-in bodies decoded from MSM's coded textures on 28 bodies, trims, banners, fonts, shaders and post effects under WebGL 2, timelines, waypoints, size, on both versions; go for 8 of 9 bodies, post and self-written shaders, post effects on a still frame). Its `bodies-sheet-*.png` show MSM's decoded codes, not game pixels.
- `b634/`: the Live views 4 (assembled structures) spike (4S1–4S10 of `docs/PLAN-live-views.md`: jigsaw assembly and processors observed on the real server with MSM's own fixture pools on both versions, read back from saved structure starts and blocks; the rule list J1–J20 for B637, the processor list for B638, the tolerance, the jar's structure files). Evidence only; the spike's code, packs and worlds were deleted.
- `b650/`: the search spike (SQ1–SQ16 of `docs/PLAN-search.md`: the corpus, index and query times in a worker, typo methods, the stemmer, 82 draft queries, answers from words, Ctrl/Cmd+K beside Monaco, the guide's packaging; a go for ADR 0124 with a lazy plugin search data entry).

The DEVLOG entries "B469" and "B470" of 2026-11-15 and "B503", "B524", "B525", "B565", "B574", "B547", "B600" and "B650" of 2026-11-22 and "B619" and "B634" of 2026-11-23 describe how each spike was run.

The drawn art of ADR 0092 (the contact sheet and the numbers chosen for the generator) is not spike evidence and lives in `docs/reference/art/` (see its README).
