# Spike evidence

Results of the throwaway spikes of vanilla parity 2, batch 2, and vanilla parity 3, kept so the briefs that build on them (B472–B485 of vanilla parity 2, batch 2; the plan was deleted at B485, its facts and limits are in ADR 0101) can read what was measured and reuse code that worked. The spike code was deleted from `engine-mod/`; these are copies. Nothing here is built, linted or shipped. Only MSM's own code and our own measurements are kept: no vanilla files, decompiled code, `javap` listings or screenshots of the game.

The decisions taken from them are in the ADR amendments, which win over anything here:

- `b469/`: traders, professions, mount storage and shoulder pets (S11–S13), measured on 26.3 and 26.4-snapshot-7. Decisions in `docs/adr/0098-traders-professions-mount-storage-and-shoulder-pets.md`, "Amendment after the spike". `spike-src/` holds the spike's Java (`spike3/`, the `SpikePoi` seam for each version) and resources; `server-*.json` are the GameTest observations by key (`S11.*`, `S12.*`, `S13.*`).
- `b470/`: custom fluids (S14), measured on both versions, with and without the 26.4 `Entity.isInWater`/`isInLava` Mixin. Decisions in `docs/adr/0099-custom-fluids.md`, "Amendment after the spike". `spike-src/` holds `spike4/` (fluids, liquid block, Mixin, client), the 26.3 `SpikeFluid` seam and resources; `server-*.json` and `client-*.json` are the observations (`client-*`: `sp.*` singleplayer, `ds.*` a dedicated server with a joined client).
- `b503/`: vanilla parity 3, batch 1 (X1–X14 of `docs/PLAN-vanilla-parity-3.md`: growing and column plants, dripleaf, lichen, project flower pots, lightning on project copper, weathering on six more types, a waterlogged container, a double chest, per-player storage, own block sounds and set types, strip, flatten, till, wax, age and burn pairs for vanilla blocks, an ageing container, spawners), measured on 26.3 and 26.4-snapshot-7. Decisions go into ADRs 0103–0105 from the B503 report. `spike-src/` holds `spike3/` (the spike mod `B503Mod` with namespace `msm_b503`, one GameTest class per question, the client GameTest), the `B503Version` seam for each version, the temporary `fabric.mod.json`, the `supports_*` tag additions, the `block_sound_set` entry and `sounds.json`; `server-*.json` are the GameTest observations by key (`X1.*` … `X14.*`), `client-*.json` the client GameTest's (screenshot names, client light, the sounds the client played). The client GameTest needs `ALSOFT_DRIVERS=null` besides the EGL and Xvfb settings, or OpenAL finds no device and no sound reaches a `SoundEventListener`.

The DEVLOG entries "B469" and "B470" of 2026-11-15 and "B503" of 2026-11-22 describe how each spike was run.

The drawn art of ADR 0092 (the contact sheet and the numbers chosen for the generator) is not spike evidence and lives in `docs/reference/art/` (see its README).
