# Search everything

One search finds everything you can make, open, change, learn or fix in MCSuperMods: Creator flows, starters, examples and templates, your own things, Minecraft's blocks, items and mobs, Rules parts, settings, commands, guide pages and Things to fix. Type plain words, misspelt words, American spelling or Minecraft slang ("blue sword", "skeliton", "armor stand", "xp"), and pick a result. Search works without the internet and without an AI key, gives the same list for the same words every time, and sends nothing anywhere.

Search matches words; it does not think. It always shows what it understood from your words and what it did not, so you can see why a result came first.

## Open the search

The same search opens from five places:

| Where | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Home** in Very easy mode | A large bar at the top, **What do you want to make?**, with a mod open or not. |
| The empty editor area and the screen with no mod open, in Easy and Advanced | The same large bar. |
| The toolbar, in every mode | A small field, **Search or make anything**. |
| **Ctrl+K** (**Cmd+K** on a Mac), or **Edit > Search…** | Opens it from anywhere. Inside a code editor Ctrl+K may belong to the editor: use the toolbar field, or press Escape first. |
| The command palette, **Ctrl+Shift+P** | Still the palette, with its look and its order; what you type there is ranked by the same search, and **Search everything…** at the end of its list opens the full search. |

Click a bar, or start typing on it, and the search opens over the window. **Escape** closes it and puts you back where you were.

Under an empty bar a line starts **Try:** with three searches to press: "blue sword", "make a pig fly" and "skyblock" in Very easy; "blue sword", "how do I add a recipe" and "export jar" in Easy; "minecraft:creeper", "export jar" and "how do I add a recipe" in Advanced.

## What you see

Results come in sections, each with three rows and **Show more** (Easy and Advanced say how many more):

| Section | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Things you can make** | Creator flows, starters, examples, templates, Studios and kinds, Rules triggers and steps, Home's tiles |
| **In your mod** | The things of the open mod, its pictures and sounds, its rules |
| **From Minecraft** | Minecraft's own blocks, items, mobs, biomes, effects, enchantments, potions, particles and sounds |
| **How to** | Sections of this guide |
| **Settings and commands** | A line of Settings, or a command you can run |
| **Things to fix** | The problems of the open mod |
| **More** | The rest, for example the technical kinds in Very easy |

Above them, **Best match** (or **Best matches**, up to three) shows the results that fit your words best, from any section. When nothing fits well, the line reads **Closest things** instead. When nothing is close at all, it says "Nothing close to that yet. Try other words, or add it to your Wishlist." with a row **Add it to my Wishlist**.

Each row has a small picture and a word saying where it comes from (Make, Example, Rule, In your mod, Minecraft, How to, Setting, Command, To fix; Easy and Advanced also say Starter, Template and Studio or Kind), its title and one sentence. Advanced shows the id too. A thing your mod's Minecraft version cannot have is still listed, greyed, with the reason ("Not on this Minecraft version."), and has no button.

In Very easy every word is plain: no ids, and none of the words template, element, starter, kind, namespace or JSON.

## What it understood

Under the field a line of chips shows what search took from your words:

- **Showing results for creeper**: a word was misspelt and search read it as another word.
- **I understood:** a chip for each colour, thing, number, name and behaviour it used ("blue", "sword", "called Frost Edge", "drops xp"). Advanced says what each one is ("colour: blue").
- **Not understood:** the words it could not place, struck through.

The × on a chip takes those words out of the field and searches again.

For a Creator flow, your words also answer its questions where they can: a colour from a colour name, a choice from its name, a number with a word beside it ("5 hearts"), the name from quotes or "called …". The row shows the answers as a sentence ("Sword, blue, called Blue Sword."). Everything you did not say keeps the flow's own default. One "that …" part can add one thing the made thing does, such as "a block that drops xp" or "a sword that sets mobs on fire" (a rule), "a sword that never wears out" (an answer of the flow) or "a sword that has fire aspect" (an enchantment made beside it); the row says what it adds.

## Look before you make

The selected result shows what it will be before anything is made. A flow, starter, example or template is built in memory with your answers and drawn as the Creator's live preview draws it; a plain tile shows first, and the picture follows a moment after you stop moving through the list. A Showcase card shows its still picture with **Play** for its clip. A thing of your mod shows its own live view, a Minecraft thing its picture from your Minecraft folder, a rule its sentence, and a guide section its first paragraph. Settings and commands have nothing to show and say what they do in one sentence.

Nothing is written to your mod until you press a button.

## The buttons

Each result has one main button, which **Enter** or a click runs; **Ctrl+Enter** (**Cmd+Enter** on a Mac) runs the second.

| Result | Main button | Others |
|---|---|---|
| A Creator flow | **Make this**: the Creator opens on the flow with your answers filled in | **Add to my mod**: made at once, one step that **Undo** takes back |
| A starter or an example | **Add to my mod**, or **Start a mod with this** when no mod is open | **See the example** |
| A template | **Add to my mod**, with a **Name** field | |
| A Studio or kind | **Make a new one** | **Show mine** |
| A thing of your mod | **Open** | **Rename**, **Find usages**, **Edit in Creator** (Very easy: **Open** and **Rename**) |
| A Minecraft thing | **Make something with it**: the flows that can use it, already answered | **Copy id** (Advanced) |
| A Rules trigger, step or condition | **Add this rule**: a new rule in your Rule book that starts with it | |
| A setting | **Open**: Settings at that line | |
| A command | **Run** | |
| A guide section | **Show me how**: the guide page, read inside the app | |
| A Thing to fix | **Fix it** when one step fixes it | **Show me** |

With no mod open, a button that makes something first starts a new mod (Very easy asks only its name, as **Start a new mod** does; Easy and Advanced ask for its folder), then makes it there.

**Show me how** opens the guide inside MCSuperMods, at the right heading, with **Back**, **Open on the web** and **Close**. Links to other guide pages stay inside; links to the web open your browser.

## Look in one place

The chips beside the field, **Make**, **My mod**, **Minecraft**, **Help** and **Commands** (not in Very easy), keep the results to one place (**My mod** also keeps Things to fix). In Advanced you can type a sign first instead: `>` for commands, `@` for your things, `#` for Minecraft ids and `?` for how to.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Down and Up | Move through the results |
| Page Down and Page Up | Move a section at a time |
| Enter | The main button (or **Show more**, the Wishlist row, the AI helper row) |
| Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter) | The second button |
| Tab | Into the preview's **Name** field and buttons |
| Escape | Close the search |

The results never take the focus from the field, so you can keep typing. A screen reader hears how many results there are and the best one. Reduced motion stops clips and list movement. In a narrow window, or zoomed in, the preview moves below the list.

## The AI helper

With an AI helper key set, the last row is always **Ask the AI helper**. It opens the helper with your words in its box; nothing is sent until you press **Send**. With no mod open it starts New project with the AI helper. Without a key, search does not offer the helper. See [The AI helper](ai-helper.md); the helper itself also searches the library first when it builds.

## Your searches stay on this computer

"Nothing you type here leaves your computer." No search, result or click is sent anywhere.

**Settings > Search > Remember my searches** (on at first) keeps your last 20 searches and the last 50 results you chose on this computer, so they come first next time. **Clear my searches** forgets them, and turning the setting off forgets them too. They are never saved in your mod, never exported and never sent to the AI helper.

## Plugins

A plugin can bring its own search words, so search finds its things. They can point only at the plugin's own things, and they are checked when the plugin loads: if any line fails the check, all its words are left out and the plugin works without them. **Settings > Plugins** says which: "Search finds it by its own words (N lines)." or "Loaded without its search words: they did not pass the check." See [Plugins](plugins.md#search-words).

## What it does not do

- **It matches words, it does not think.** A way of saying something it does not know may find nothing. The chips show what it understood; try other words, or ask the AI helper.
- **English only**, British and American.
- **One "that …" part.** It uses the first one, and makes at most one rule or one extra flow from it; the rest is struck through. Use the Rule book or the AI helper for more.
- **Rules need the Fabric mod jar**, as every rule does; a vanilla pack export leaves them out.
- **Minecraft's own names and pictures need your Minecraft folder.** Without it you see names made from ids and plain pictures, and the line "Set up your Minecraft folder to see Minecraft's own names and pictures."
- **It cannot make what MCSuperMods cannot make.** "Make a pig fly" makes a new flying mob called Flying Pig; Minecraft's own pig stays as it is. A behaviour with nothing behind it is struck through, and the Wishlist is there for it.
- **Answers come only from your words.** A number with nothing beside it is not used; everything else is the flow's default, shown in the preview. **Make this** lets you change it all in the Creator first.
- **Only the selected result is drawn**, and some take longer (a Wood set, a world): a plain tile shows first.
- **Things MCSuperMods does not have yet are not found**; you get the closest thing that exists.
- **Typos:** one wrong letter in words of four to seven letters, two in longer words; words of three letters or fewer must be exact, and a misspelling that is itself a word ("bow" for "box") is not corrected.
- **Your mod is searched by names, ids, kinds and rule sentences**, not by every value inside every file, and not inside functions or scripts.
- **On a very large mod** the first search after opening it can take a moment.
- **Settings and commands have no picture**, only a sentence.
- **The guide inside the app is text only.**
