# Very easy mode

Very easy mode is for making a mod with no code and no files to look at. You pick what to make from big tiles, answer a few questions, press **Play** and see it in Minecraft. Everything is in plain words: no ids, no JSON, no file lists.

There are three modes, and you can change between them at any time without losing anything:

| Mode | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Very easy** | Big picture tiles, Play and plain words. No code and no files to look at. |
| **Easy** | Your things first, the files behind them folded away, the forms only. |
| **Advanced** | Every file, kind ids and Raw JSON. |

The first time MCSuperMods starts, the Welcome asks **How do you want to work?** and starts in Very easy; you can choose Easy or Advanced there. After that, use the row **Very easy · Easy · Advanced** in the toolbar, or **View > Very Easy Mode**, **Easy Mode** or **Advanced Mode**. The older switch beside the row still toggles Easy and Advanced. Your choice is remembered.

Nothing is hidden for good. Very easy only hides things: a hand-made event, a technical element or a file you made in Advanced mode still exists and still runs. If something in your mod happens and you cannot see why, switch to Easy or Advanced to look.

## The Very easy window

The toolbar has only what you need: **Home**, the search field **Search or make anything**, Undo, Redo, **Try it**, **Play** and **Reload** with the game's status, **Share my mod**, the AI helper, Help and the mode row. There is no sidebar, no Problems tab and no target in the status bar. Anything wrong appears in **Things to fix**.

## What do you want to make?

The large bar at the top of Home, **What do you want to make?**, is there with a mod open or not. Type what you want in your own words, such as "blue sword", "make a pig fly" or "skyblock", and pick a result: **Make this** opens the questions with your answers already filled in, and **Add to my mod** makes it straight away (Undo takes it back). The same search finds your own things (**Open**), Minecraft's things (**Make something with it**), Things to fix (**Fix it**) and how-to pages of this guide (**Show me how**). Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) opens it from anywhere. Nothing you type leaves your computer. See [Search everything](search.md).

## Start a new mod

On Home, **Start a new mod** asks one question: **What is your mod called?** Press **Start** and the mod is saved in a folder of that name in `Documents/MCSuperMods`. Under the question it says where: "It is saved in …". **Change folder** picks another place. You never see a namespace or an id; MCSuperMods makes them from the name. **Open a mod** opens one you made before, **Surprise me** makes a random mod for you ([Surprise me](#surprise-me)), and **Your mods** lists the recent ones.

## Your mods

With no mod open, **Your mods** sits under the three buttons, above **See what you can make**. Each mod you opened lately is a card with its picture (its `pack.png` if it has one, else its first item or block picture, else its initials), its name, how many things it has and when you last opened it ("Opened 2 hours ago"). **Open** opens it; the **…** menu has **Show in folder** and **Remove from this list** (the mod itself stays on your computer). The eight most recent show; **See all my mods** lists the rest. A mod whose folder was moved or deleted says "This mod's folder is not there any more." with **Remove**. The arrow keys move between the cards.

## More ideas

Under **See what you can make**, **More ideas** goes on for ever: each card is a mod Surprise me would make, with its name, a line on what is in it, the names of its things and a picture of them. Scroll and more keep coming. **Make this** asks **What is your mod called?** (filled in with the card's name) and makes exactly that mod, as **Surprise me** would. **Shuffle** shows other ideas; without it the list is the same each time you come back. Hover a card to see a short clip of its blocks and mobs; the setting **Play showcase clips** and reduced motion work as they do for the cards above. The search finds both sections ("more ideas", "all my mods").

## See what you can make

Under the three buttons on Home, **See what you can make** shows thirteen cards of things to make: a set of wood, a pet, a crop, a machine, a vehicle, a portal, sparkles, a trader, a liquid, a lucky block and three worlds of your own (islands, tall mountains, caves). Hover a card to see a short clip. **Make this** starts the card's flow, asking only **What is your mod called?** when no mod is open, and **See the example** opens or copies the finished example. With a mod open, the cards sit between the tiles and **My things**, then fold to **Show what you can make** once your mod has things; **Hide what you can make** folds them again. **Settings > Showcase > Play showcase clips** turns the moving pictures off. See [See what you can make](showcase.md).

## Make something

With a mod open, Home shows **Make something** with these tiles, under headings:

| Heading | Tile | What it opens |
|---|---|---|
| | **Surprise me** | A random mod to start from (see [Surprise me](#surprise-me)) |
| Blocks and items | **Make a block** | A new block, an ore, stone with stairs, lights, a bed, a cake, a sign, plants, a pot or a liquid |
| | **Make a chest or machine** | A crate that stores things, or a machine that cooks or grinds them |
| | **Make an item** | Something to throw, a totem that saves you, wings to glide or a bag that holds things |
| | **Make a tool or weapon** | A sword, a whole set of tools, or a new enchantment |
| | **Make armour** | Helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots of a new material, or a new trim |
| | **Make food** | Something to eat or drink |
| | **Make a plant or tree** | A crop to grow, a tree, or a whole set of wood |
| | **Make a recipe** | Ready-made crafting, smelting and stonecutting recipes to change |
| Vehicles | **Make a vehicle** | Cars, tanks, planes and boats you can drive. It asks **What kind?** (Car, Tank, Plane, Boat) and **What is it called?**, then opens the vehicle with **Try it** and **Play** offered |
| Creatures | **Make a mob** | A new creature, a flying or swimming one, or a boss |
| | **Make a pet or animal** | A pet to tame, a farm animal or something to ride |
| | **Make a trader** | A mob that trades with you, a new job for villagers, or new clothes for them |
| Magic | **Make a potion or effect** | A potion to brew or a new effect |
| | **Make sparkles** | Particles of your own around a block or from an item |
| | **Make a magic wand** | A wand that heals, throws you up, calls lightning or gives an effect |
| Worlds | **Make a portal** | A portal frame, or a whole new world to travel to |
| | **Make a world** | Islands, tall mountains, one biome everywhere, caves only or an empty sky; or a new biome |
| | **Make a building** | A small village of huts and lamp posts that appears in forests |
| | **Change drops and trades** | An extra drop from a mob, block or chest, or a new villager trade |
| Games | **Make a game** | A Skyblock, one block, sky islands, a lucky block, an adventure map, an arena, a race or goals |
| Looks and sounds | **Change how Minecraft looks** | A new skin for a wolf, cat or pig, or a new look for a vanilla item |
| | **Make a painting or pattern** | A painting of your own picture, a pattern for banners or a picture for pots |
| | **Make a sound or music** | A music disc with your own song, or a goat horn call |
| | **Paint a picture** | Draw a new picture, pixel by pixel |
| Rules and help | **Make things happen** | Rules: when something happens, do something. No code needed. |
| | **Build with the AI helper** | Say what you want and let the AI helper plan it; you look at what it made, then **Add to my mod** ([the AI helper](ai-helper.md)) |

Most tiles open the [Creator](creator.md) on the right question page, or on its list of pages when a tile offers several. **Make a recipe**, **Make a building**, **Make a world** and **Change how Minecraft looks** also offer ready-made content: they open a short list, where a question page opens the Creator and a ready-made choice is added to your mod at once (one step, so **Undo** takes it back). **Make things happen** opens your [rule book](rules.md); the first time it makes one. A tile whose pages do not exist for your Minecraft version is not shown.

A vehicle opens on a short page, not the full Studio: **How fast**, **Needs fuel** (with **What fuel?**), **Has a cannon** (for a Tank), **How many seats** and **Colour**. In **Make things happen** the Rules offer **A player presses a key** (**Press the key you want**), **A player lets go of a key**, **A player holds a key**, **Someone gets in the vehicle**, **Someone gets out**, **The vehicle fires**, **The vehicle is destroyed** and **Show a number on screen** (**As a bar or as text?**, **Where on screen?** with a picture of nine spots and "above the hotbar"). See [Vehicles](vehicles.md), [Keys and the screen](keys-and-hud.md) and [Rules](rules.md#vehicles-keys-and-the-screen). The keys, vehicles and on-screen elements you make show under **My things**, and Things to fix explains problems in plain words.

Two tiles show a short list of **pictures** when they open the Creator, so you can pick by looking. **Make a block** offers **Lights** ("Torches, lanterns, chains and candles."), **Bed** ("A bed or sleeping bag."), **Cake** ("Eat it a slice at a time."), **Sign** ("Write on it. Comes with a whole set of wood."), **Bush with berries** ("A plant to grow and pick.", then **Bush with berries** in the flow), **Ages like copper** ("Blocks that slowly turn green, and stay as they are when waxed."), **Grows in long strands** ("Vines with glowing berries, kelp, or a moss that spreads over any side."), **Grows tall like sugar cane** ("Cane, cactus, bamboo, or a leaf that tips when you stand on it.") and **A flower pot** ("A pot of your own for the flowers you pick."). **Make an item** offers **A bag that holds things** ("Opens when you right-click it."), beside the Glider flow. **Make a painting or pattern** opens the Painting, Banner pattern and Pot pattern flows, and **Make armour** the Gear set and Armour trim flows. The pick opens the Creator on the right flow, and the flow's card shows the pick's words ([Block types, families and plants](block-types-and-plants.md), [Bags and bundles](blocks-and-items.md#bags-and-bundles), [The Creator](creator.md)). **Make your mod do something** opens the Custom command, Creative tab and Game rule flows ([Commands and creative tabs](commands-and-tabs.md)); a command or tab you make shows under **My things** as **Command** or **Creative tab**.

**Make a world** shows five pictures first: **Islands** ("Islands in the sea, as big as you like."), **Tall mountains** ("Towering peaks with snow on top."), **One biome everywhere** ("A whole world of one biome, such as an endless cherry grove."), **Caves only** ("Caverns and tunnels, lit by glowing rock.") and, in place of **Empty sky with a start island**, **Skyblock** ("Start on a small island with a chest, in an empty sky."), then the Dimension flow and the Custom biome. Each picture opens its Creator flow, which asks a few questions such as **How big are the islands?**, **How much sea?**, **How tall?** or **Which biome?**, and its **Map** tab draws your world as you answer. The world becomes a World Type: choose it under **More > World Type** when you create a world in Minecraft. See [Worldgen](worldgen.md#make-a-world-in-a-few-questions) and [The live world map](live-world.md).

**Make a game** shows eight pictures: **Skyblock** ("Start on a small island with a chest, in an empty sky."), **One block** ("One magic block that turns into the next each time you break it."), **Sky islands** ("A world of islands floating in the sky."), **Lucky block** ("A block that does something random when you break it."), **Adventure map** ("A map with a welcome, checkpoints and goals."), **Arena game** ("Teams, points and rounds in an arena."), **Race** ("A timed race through checkpoints.") and **Goals** ("A list of goals in the advancements screen."). Each opens its Creator flow, whose questions are in [Game modes](game-modes.md#make-a-game-in-a-few-questions). The Studios of the game's pieces show only their sections here, with no ids. Press **Play my game** in the Play menu to play it in a world of its own ([Playing and testing](playing-and-testing.md#play-my-game)), and look at it in [Try it](try-it.md#games) on the grounds **Empty sky** and **One of my buildings**.

In **Make things happen** the Rules also offer, in the same plain words, **A player places a block** (**Which block?**) with **Stop the placing**; **Something explodes** with **Stop the explosion** and **Keep the blocks safe**; **A player types the command** (then pick the command, shown as `/name`); **A player gets on something** and **A player gets off something** with **Ride something** and **Get off**; and the checks **Someone is riding** and **Something is facing a way** (north, east, south, west, up or down), with **The way it faces** for messages and **In front of here** for a place ([Rules](rules.md#placing-explosions-commands-and-riding)).

**Make a trader** opens a short list of three pages: **Wandering merchant** ("A mob that trades with you."), **Village job** ("A new job for villagers, with its workbench.") and **Villager look** ("New clothes for the villagers of a biome."). The merchant asks **What is it called?**, **What does it look like?** (the mob pictures), then **What does it sell?** (pick an item) and **For how many emeralds?**; the job asks **What is the job called?**, the colours of its apron and workbench and its first trades. Both create with their defaults, so you can press **Create** at once. **Make a block** also offers **Fluid** ("A liquid of your own, with its bucket."). See [Villagers, traders and pets that carry things](villagers-and-traders.md) and [Fluids](fluids.md). In the pet and mount pages, **Rides on your shoulder** and **Carries a chest** are single switches.

In the Creator, **Pick for me** fills in every question with random answers that work; change any of them before you press **Create**.

## Draw it for me

Next to a picture, **Draw it for me** draws it in a Minecraft-like style: pick a **Colour**, pick one of the six variations (**New looks** shows six more) and **Use this picture**. The mod's own things are already drawn when they are made, so this is for choosing another look; **Edit > Undo** puts the old picture back. A mob has **Draw its skin**. MCSuperMods draws the pictures itself and never copies them from Minecraft ([Drawing pictures](pictures.md)).

## Surprise me

Not sure what to make? **Surprise me** makes a small random mod to start from. It is the first tile on Home, a button on Home when no mod is open, **File > Surprise Me…**, and "Surprise me: make a random mod" in the command palette, in every mode.

Every picture in a surprise is drawn in a Minecraft-like style ([Drawing pictures](pictures.md)). A surprise has a theme, such as Ruby, Frost, Ember, Moss, Sky or Void, and three to five things named after it: always an ore with its gem (or a set of tools and armour with its ore), then a few of a block, a weapon, a food, a mob or pet, a potion or effect, a plant or tree, sparkles and a portal. Sometimes it also has a rule, for example "When a player breaks Ruby Ore, 25 times in 100 an extra Ruby drops." The pictures are placeholders in the theme's colours: paint over them later.

The **Surprise me** window shows everything before anything is made: each thing with its pictures and what it makes, and the rule. Then:

- **Make it** makes it. With a mod open, the things are added to it, with new names if yours already has things of the same name. With no mod open, it starts a new mod named after the theme ("Ruby Mod") in `Documents/MCSuperMods`.
- **Roll again** shows another random mod.
- **Change theme** keeps the same things in another theme.
- **Seed** is the surprise's number: the same seed always gives the same mod, so you can type a friend's seed to get theirs.

Everything a surprise makes is one step, so **Undo** takes all of it back. Each thing is a normal Creator set: open its card in My things, or use **Edit in Creator** to change its answers.

## My things

Below the tiles, **My things** has a card for each thing you made: a set from the Creator (a gear set is one card), or a loose block, item, mob, effect, potion, portal, **Village job**, **Liquid** or rule book, with its picture. A **Village job** card (its profession, workbench block and trades are one card) opens at the job, and a **Liquid** card opens the liquid, not its block or bucket. Choose a card to open it in its editor; the editor shows the parts that matter and keeps the rest away. Under the cards, **Particles and sounds** has a card for each particle and each sound of your mod, with **Play** on a sound's card; choose one to see it or hear it ([Live views](live-views.md#looks-and-sounds)). A **Rules** section comes first in the block, item, mob and effect editors, so the quickest way to make a thing do something is to open it and add a rule ([Rules](rules.md)).

A card's menu has:

- **Edit in Creator**, for a set made in the Creator;
- **Rename**, which changes the name players see, never the id;
- **Duplicate**, which makes a copy named "… (copy)";
- **Delete**, which removes the whole set.

Each is one step, so **Undo** takes it back.

Rename only changes the shown name, so it never breaks a world. **Delete** asks first when you have already played with the thing or shared it (Play, Reload, Test in Minecraft, an install or an export): worlds that already have it lose it, so its blocks disappear and its items and creatures are gone. Choose **Delete anyway** to go on, or **Cancel** to keep it. **Undo** brings it back in MCSuperMods, but not in those worlds.

## Things to fix

When something stops your mod from playing, **Things to fix (N)** lists it in plain words, with the thing's name: for example a picture that is missing, or a step in a rule that cannot work. Each line has:

- **Show me**, which opens the thing at the right place;
- **Fix it**, where one click can fix it, for example **Paint the picture** to open the painter on the missing picture or **Remove this step** for a rule step;
- **Ask the helper**, once the [AI helper](ai-helper.md#ask-the-helper-about-a-problem) is set up: it opens the helper with the problem attached and sends nothing until you press **Send**.

The newest things are explained in the same plain way, for example:

- a block that ages into one that is gone: "<name> ages into a block that is not in your mod any more. Pick another one.";
- a sign with no writing screen picture: "<name> has no picture for the screen you write on. Pick the sign's picture again in its Textures.";
- a bag that stacks above one: "<name> holds things inside, so it can only stack to 1. Set its stack size to 1.";
- a berry bush or a potted plant that points at something missing: "<name> gives a berry that is not an item in your mod or in Minecraft. Pick another item.";
- a command with a name that Minecraft or another of your commands already uses: "<name> has a name that Minecraft or another of your commands already uses. Pick another.";
- a creative tab whose icon does not exist: "The creative tab <name> shows a picture of something that does not exist. Pick another.";
- a trader that borrows a list of trades that does not exist: "<name> trades from a list of trades that does not exist. Pick other trades for it.";
- a trader that borrows trades only some villagers may offer: "<name> borrows trades that only some villagers may offer, so it offers fewer of them or none. Give it trades of its own.";
- a monster that trades: "<name> is a monster, so it does not trade at night. That is allowed." (a tip, not a problem);
- a village job with no work block of its own: "<name> needs a work block of your own. Make a block and pick it.", or one whose work block belongs to Minecraft or another job: "<name> uses a block that already belongs to Minecraft or to another job as its work block. Pick a block of your own.";
- a shoulder pet that is too wide or not tameable: "<name> is too wide to sit on a shoulder. Make it narrower, or let it stay on the ground." and "<name> can only sit on a shoulder if it is an animal that can be tamed.";
- a mob with a chest bigger than one chest: "<name> holds more than the one chest put on it. That is allowed." (a tip);
- a liquid whose bucket has the name of something else: "The bucket of <name> has the same name as another thing in your mod. Rename one of them.";
- a liquid that looks further for a way down than it can spread: "<name> looks further for a way down than it can spread. Make it look less far, or spread further.";
- a liquid missing a picture: "<name> is missing one of its pictures, so it looks like plain water or lava there.";
- a liquid that behaves like nothing: "<name> behaves like nothing, so players see through it as through air. Make it behave like water or lava.";
- a liquid that runs something that is gone when you are in it: "<name> runs something when you are in it that is not there any more. Pick what happens again.";
- a rule step from another moment: "A rule in <name> uses a step or a check that only works with another "When" (such as "Stop the placing" when a player places a block).".

If there is nothing to fix it says "Everything is ready to play." Less serious notes are folded under **Tips**. A problem MCSuperMods has no words for yet reads "Something in <thing> needs fixing", with **Show details** for the original message.

If a mistake stops the mod from being built, **Play** and **Share my mod** send nothing to the game and say so here.

## Play and Reload

**Play** saves your mod, builds it and starts Minecraft in a test world with your things in chests and in your hotbar. **Reload** sends later changes to the running game. Both are in the Very easy toolbar. The whole story, including Restart, is in [Playing and testing](playing-and-testing.md).

## Try it

**Try it** in the toolbar (or F6) opens a small test area inside MCSuperMods, with no Minecraft needed. Your blocks and spawn eggs are in the hotbar: walk or fly around, place and break blocks, open doors and watch your mobs wander. It is a quick preview, not the game: what it cannot show is listed under "What happened" with "Only in Minecraft", and **Play in Minecraft** is always at the top for the real check ([Try it](try-it.md)). A trader you made shows its trading screen there, a liquid's bucket pours a pool that does not flow, and your plants grow with **Grow**. Your scripts and rules run there too, in a sandbox, and say what they did in **What happened**; a pass in Try it is not a pass in the game, so press **Play** for the real check.

## Share my mod

**Share my mod** builds the Fabric mod jar with the usual settings and saves it where you choose. It then says: "Your mod is saved. Give the file to friends: they put it in their mods folder." **Where is it?** shows the file. Friends need Fabric Loader and Fabric API, like for any Fabric mod; the jar carries MCSuperMods' small runtime, so nothing else is needed. (The same file is **Fabric mod (.jar)…** in the Export menu in the other modes; see [Blocks and items](blocks-and-items.md#export-a-fabric-mod-jar).)

To share the mod with everyone, not only your friends, add the project to the [MCSuperMods community repository](https://github.com/MEZTKING/MCSuperMods-Community): copy your project folder (the one with `msm.project.json` in it) into its `mods/` folder with a short README, a picture and a licence, and open a pull request. Its checks open and export every shared mod on both Minecraft versions, and refuse Minecraft's own pictures and sounds. The repository also has every example of MCSuperMods as a project you can open; its `CONTRIBUTING.md` explains each step.

## What Very easy leaves out

Very easy shows the question pages and the sections that matter and hides the rest of a Studio: the events editor and the triggers, the Advanced switch, Raw JSON and every id. A field that names a thing shows its name instead: an item, block, mob, sound, particle, effect or dimension is a button with its name that opens the picture picker, a biome, creative tab or potion is a list of names, and a model or picture is shown by its name only (change a picture with **Draw…**, **Paint** or **Make with AI**). The made models of a block, a potion's name key and its ids are left out, and an example's **Notes** keep only the sentences that read in plain words. A command's editor shows how it is typed, its name, what follows it, who can use it, what it does and its message, with **Rules** in place of its events. If you need a field that is hidden, switch to Easy.
