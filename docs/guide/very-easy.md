# Very easy, Easy and Advanced mode

There are no modes any more: MCSuperMods has one way of working for everyone, in plain words, with the rarer settings under **More options** at the bottom of each part. Home, Make something, My things, Things to fix, Draw it for me and Share my mod are there for everyone, and the ids, every setting and the raw text are under **More options**. The toolbar is one row, with everything else in its **More** menu. See [Plain words and More options](more-options.md).
